# Comfort (すみごこち / Comfy Level) as a state that moves over days: noticing, daily wishes, food, friendship and patches (Pokémon Pokopia, state as of Ver. 2.0.1, Nov 2026)

Evidence labels on every finding: **official** (patch notes or in-game text), **community-tested** (a named player measured it in game), **guide** (a guide site states it without a published test), **unverified** (one comment, anecdote or a hypothesis its own author calls unproven). "Search summary" means the page itself could not be read (proxy or site refused) and only the search engine's summary was seen. Dates are page dates; the version is inferred from the date (1.0.1 on 4 Mar 2026, 1.0.2 on 18 Mar per Game8 JP (Serebii says 4 Mar), 1.0.3 9 Apr, 1.0.4 23 Apr, 1.1.0 10 Jun JP / 9 Jun EN, 1.1.1 19 Jun, 2.0.0 5 Aug, 2.0.1 8 Sep).

Terms: すみごこち どう？ = "How's your comfort level?"; ほしいもの = the thing it wants; きゅうけい / おもちゃ / かざりつけ = Relaxation / Toy / Decoration item tags; 環境レベル = Environment Level; 仲良し度 / なかよし度 / 親密度 = friendship; すみか = home; すみかなし = no home; ランク names lowest to highest: すみかなし (No home), びみょう (Iffy), ふつう (Average), ちょっといい感じ (Nice), めちゃイイ感じ (Great), 最高 (Awesome).

Corrections to the earlier notes and report found while re-reading the primary sources:
- The fresh resident that started at びみょう in ぼってぃ's test is **パピモッチ (Fidough)**, not Swirlix (ペロッパフ) — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440).
- 攻略大百科's flavour table lists ヒメリのみ and ラムのみ under **普通 (plain)**, not spicy; the earlier "table-layout slip" reading is confirmed — [攻略大百科 3509](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/3509).
- The report sentence "Food left on a plate near the habitat, or berries on nearby trees, gets eaten automatically" cites GameWith 545675, but that page only says to hand food over with 「これ見て！」. The plate part is supported by Game8 JP 767513, Nintendo Life and the plate's item text; **no source found supports the tree-berry part** — [GameWith 545675](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/545675); [Game8 JP 767513](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767513).
- ぼってぃ's floor hypothesis ("once ふつう, never below ふつう") was **broken by the tester's own later test**: Pokémon that had been ちょっといい感じ came back at ふつう (Pichu) and びみょう (Fidough) after losing their homes and being re-homed — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440).

## 1. What kinds of wish 「すみごこち どう？」 can produce, and how they are worded

### Takeaway
The daily wish names a **genre, not an item**: one of the three furniture tags (かざりつけ / おもちゃ / きゅうけい), a food (by flavour, or just food when hungry), or an environment change (brighter, darker and the like). Any item carrying the named tag satisfies it. No source shows a wish that names a specific item, and only one unverified comment hints that a wish can combine a favourite category with a tag.

### Cited Findings
- **[Guide, Game8 JP, 2026-03-31]** Ask 「すみごこち どう？」 to learn "ほしいものやしてほしいこと" (what it wants, or wants done). The worked screenshot is captioned 「かざりつけ」を欲しがっている (it wants a "decoration"). "ほしいものと同じジャンルの物が必要になります" (you need an item of the **same genre** as the wanted thing), bought in the shop's かざりつけ genre or crafted. Steps: place it inside the home's range (R-stick shows it); the Pokémon walks over and reacts; "対象のポケモンがすみかから離れている場合は置いてくれたことを知らないので、直接報告する必要があります" (if it is away from home it doesn't know, so you must tell it directly); comfort then shows **one rank higher** (example びみょう → ふつう). "すみごこちをよくするためにしてほしいことは、1日1つまでしかわからない" (you can learn only one thing per day). Things beyond the asked wish also raise comfort (check the Pokédex favourites and liked environment) — [Game8 JP 767513](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767513)
- **[Guide, Game8 JP, updated 2026-11-26]** "ポケモンによって家具を設置する・周囲を暗くする等求めるものが変わ[ります]" (depending on the Pokémon, what it asks for varies: placing furniture, darkening the surroundings, etc.). Separately, environment-preference **requests** such as 「明るすぎる」 (too bright) and 「暗すぎる」 (too dark) are easy and raise comfort when done — [Game8 JP 767551](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767551)
- **[Guide, 攻略大百科, updated 2026-09-01]** Put "言われた通りのもの" (exactly what it said) in its home. If the Pokémon asks for 「休憩」 (rest), place furniture marked きゅうけい (beds, chairs). If **food is shown**, hand over food of that **flavour** directly ("たべものが表示された場合、該当する味の食べ物を直接手渡す"). If told brightness is lacking ("明るさが足りないと言われた場合"), add a light such as the mushroom lamp; for a dark-lover, add a roof over the home or remove lights from the house. A Pokémon can't use a bed or chair **smaller than itself**, so bigger ones are safer — [攻略大百科 3509](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/3509)
- **[Guide, Serebii]** Toy page: "These items are the items marked as Toy and can be used to boost a Pokémon's comfy level **when they ask for an item of this category**"; the Relaxation page says the same of Relaxation items; furniture "has the Relaxation and Decoration flags which some Pokémon may request to increase their Comfy Level". Asking "will often give a specific desire such as food, or a decoration for their habitat" — [Serebii Toy](https://serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items/toy.shtml); [Serebii Relaxation](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items/relaxation.shtml); [Serebii Furniture](https://serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/furniture.shtml); [Serebii Environment & Comfy Level](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/environmentlevel.shtml)
- **[Guide, Nintendo Life, 2026-07-29]** "Whenever you ask a Pokémon 'How's your comfort level?', they'll not only tell you how they're feeling, but they'll also give you a little hint as to what they're missing. This could be a type of furniture — from Decoration to Toys to Relaxation — to a type of food and flavouring." The current rank is the text "highlighted in orange" — [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/pokemon-pokopia-how-to-raise-comfort-levels)
- **[Guide, search summary]** Hints "are usually broad categories rather than specific items … if a Pokemon says it wants 'a toy,' any craftable item tagged as a toy will satisfy the request"; Bulbapedia describes a thought bubble "displaying a request for something that will raise that Pokémon's comfort level" — [GamerGuides](https://www.gamerguides.com/pokemon-pokopia/guide/getting-started/tips-and-tricks/raising-comfort-levels); [Bulbapedia: Comfort level](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Comfort_level) (search summaries)
- **[Community-tested, ぽこポケ学会]** The wish comes once a day; a hungry Pokémon's wish is food first ("空腹だと、食べ物が優先される"), so give food before asking. If it names no wish ("要望を何も言ってくれない場合"), it often turns 最高 after time passes or an area change — [ぽこポケ学会: 家と住み心地](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/)
- **[Community-tested, ぼってぃ, 2026-03-16, Ver. 1.0.1/1.0.2]** After lighting a dark-lover's room with mushroom lamps, asking produced "希望通りの反応" (the complaint the tester expected) while the rank stayed ふつう. Items that were **not** the genre named in the wish still raised comfort once the Pokémon noticed them — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Unverified, one reader comment on Game8 JP]** "ブースターのほしいもの、ほのおを感じる・おもちゃで喜んでくれました" (Flareon's wanted thing was satisfied by a fire-feeling toy), which hints that a wish may name a favourite category as well as a tag — [Game8 JP 775378 comments](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/775378)
- **[Official, Ver. 2.0.0]** Fixed: "ポケモンからの「おねがいごと」で、そのポケモンが好みではないアイテムを要求されることがある" (requests could ask for items the Pokémon dislikes). This is the おねがいごと (request) system, not the comfort wish — [Game8 JP update log](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/771132); [Serebii patches](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/patch.shtml)

### Inferences
- A planner can model the wish as a draw from {rest, toy, decoration, food (flavour), environment fix}, and the layout decides whether it can be met at once. If a home already holds a rest, a toy and a decoration in range and its environment matches, the only wish the layout can't pre-empt is food, which matches the advice to feed before asking.
- Because any item with the named tag satisfies the wish, the planner only needs item tags (きゅうけい / おもちゃ / かざりつけ) and size for rest items, not per-item wish data.
- The size rule for rest items (no bed or chair smaller than the Pokémon) is a static layout check the planner can run.

### Gaps
- No exact in-game wording of a wish line was found in Japanese or English (no screenshot transcription); Bulbapedia's page, where it might be, is 403 and a stub per the search summary.
- No source lists every wish genre, says whether a wish can name a favourite category (e.g. "something fire-feeling") or a temperature or humidity change, or says whether a rest wish names a size.
- Whether the day's wish is re-rolled once fulfilled (a second wish the same day) is untested; every guide says one per day.

## 2. How far comfort moves in a day, what triggers it, whether it drifts, and how fast it falls

### Takeaway
The daily wish is worth exactly **one rank**, but it is not the only trigger. Any noticed change re-scores the Pokémon at once: new items, food, environment, a new home and friendship actions. One tester took a resident from homeless to 最高 in one day and another from びみょう to ちょっといい感じ in one session. Rises therefore track **noticing**, not time. Falls are sticky: environment worsening while housed failed to drop rank in every test, while losing the home and being re-homed did reset it. No source measures a fall rate.

### Cited Findings
- **[Community-tested, ぼってぃ, 2026-03-16]** "すみごこちを上げるためには、期間（時間）よりも、ポケモンが認識するかどうかが重要" (what matters is not time but whether the Pokémon recognises the change). A Pokémon off screen, or on screen but not noticing, gets no update. Leading the Pokémon to the item and dismissing it there triggers the noticing motion; changing town (a reload) also seems to work. "環境、食べ物、すみごこち確認は半強制でポケモンが認識してくれるので即時反映される" (environment, food and the comfort question force recognition, so they apply at once). Placed decorations show 「見てみるよ」, then 「ありがとう」 after it looks — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Community-tested, ぼってぃ]** Same-day multi-rank moves: (a) homeless → 最高 in one day with every condition ready (favourite food already given), by moving it in and making it notice the items; (b) Pichu given a bare grass habitat went straight to ふつう ("即時反映", applied instantly; possibly a dark reading from roof shade); (c) Fidough given a new home was びみょう, rose to ふつう after its favourite food, then to ちょっといい感じ after a toy (block set) was placed between two homes, which also lifted the area to Lv2 — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Unverified hypothesis, ぼってぃ]** A Pokémon that has been 最高 or めちゃイイ感じ before may climb back fast to that "past best" (天井, ceiling) but need days, "最大3日？" (up to 3?), to exceed it; the tester also floats a hidden accumulated score that slowly lifts long-time residents above ふつう. Neither is tested — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Community-tested, ぼってぃ]** Things that did **not** move rank: a 「・・・」 talk (the heart appeared, the rank stayed ふつう); plain food (Leppa) to a ふつう Pichu, then its favourite (sweet beans) the same day, possibly because the day's meal was used; moving it to a brighter spot (day and night); blocking the path to the Pokémon Center; lighting a dark-lover's room; a reload; walling a ふつう Torchic's house in ice; moving a ちょっといい感じ Machoke to plain grass in a dark room it dislikes (still ちょっといい感じ) — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Community-tested, ぼってぃ]** Re-homing does reset: Pichu and Fidough were both ちょっといい感じ before the tester destroyed homes to reset the town; once re-homed they read ふつう and びみょう, which broke the floor hypothesis. Destroying a house showed すみかなし on the PC at once, but the Environment Level did not fall until later, apparently after the Pokémon noticed or a load — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Guide, Game8 JP 767513; 攻略大百科 3509; GameWith 545675]** The wish is fixed at one per Pokémon per real day, resetting at **05:00**; fulfilling it raises comfort one rank ("1段階上昇") — [Game8 JP 767513](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767513); [攻略大百科 3509](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/3509); [GameWith 545675](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/545675)
- **[Official, Ver. 1.1.0]** "「環境レベル」の詳細画面で、その日の「すみごこち」のお願いを叶えたポケモンが分かるようにしました" (the Environment Level details screen now shows which Pokémon had their comfort wish granted that day); 攻略大百科 says the mark is a 「✅」 — [Game8 JP update log](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/771132); [攻略大百科 Ver.1.1.0](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/5597)
- **[Guide, 攻略大百科, 2026-10-28, Ver. 2.0.1]** "ポケモンとの仲良し度を高める行動をすると、お願いとは別にすみごこちが上がることが確認できています" (friendship-raising actions are confirmed to raise comfort separately from the wish) — [攻略大百科 4899](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/4899)
- **[Guide, Game8 EN, 2026-03-08]** "Finishing the mini games will increase the comfort level of the Pokemon involved … for most of these games, this is only applicable once a day" (Hide-and-Seek, Jump Rope, Look That Way, Quizzes). Look That Way: "Completing a minigame will increase the comfort level of the Pokemon that you played with." Chat page: "Most interactions with Pokemon will yield in increasing their comfort level, regardless of their outcome!" (likely conflates comfort with friendship) — [Game8 EN mini games](https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Pokopia/archives/585464); [Game8 EN Look That Way](https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Pokopia/archives/585564); [Game8 EN chat](https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Pokopia/archives/586354)
- **[Guide, Game8 EN, 2026-03-05]** Completing requests raises the requester's comfort; playing games raises it; "A Pokemon's comfort level increases faster once they move out of their original habitat and into a house"; a Pokémon refuses a house containing an item it dislikes — [Game8 EN comfort](https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Pokopia/archives/583142)
- **[Guide, Serebii]** Scrub (Ver. 2.0.0 / Bubbly Basin): "if a Pokémon with Scrub follows you, it **may** help clean other Pokémon boosting their happiness and environment level slightly"; the environment page says it "boosts its comfy level and friendship". No daily cap is stated anywhere found — [Serebii Scrub](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/pokedex/specialty/scrub.shtml); [Serebii Environment & Comfy Level](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/environmentlevel.shtml)
- **[Guide, Serebii]** "If you then continue giving them gifts every day and talking to them, then their comfy level will increase further"; a house raises it "quicker" than a habitat — [Serebii Environment & Comfy Level](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/environmentlevel.shtml)
- **[Community-tested, ぽこポケ学会]** Item counting is spatial: comfort counts items from 4 below to 3 above the door's height (−4 to +3), and items the Pokémon can't reach count for nothing ("ポケモンがアクセス不可能だと、住心地に影響を与えない"). Light sources and other environment items count only inside the house and above or below the door (floor −1 to +3; "検証不十分", not fully tested) — [ぽこポケ学会](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/)
- **[Guide] Causes of falls named by guides:** disliked things near the home (fire near Grass types, water near Fire types) — [攻略大百科 3509](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/3509); [Serebii](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/environmentlevel.shtml). Using furniture, toys or food it doesn't like — [攻略大百科 4899](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/4899). A comfort item left broken, and outdoor fires put out by rain (roof them) — [Game8 JP 767551](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767551)
- **[Official, Ver. 2.0.0]** "家具がなくなって悲しんでいるポケモンが、同じ家具を置き直した時に、より気づきやすくしました" (a Pokémon saddened by furniture being removed now notices more easily when the same furniture is put back). So removal produces a "sad" state and noticing applies on the way down as well — [Game8 JP update log](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/771132)
- **[Official, Ver. 1.1.0 fix]** "特定の状況で、ポケモンが苦手なブロックを撤去しても、「すみごこち」が上がらないことがある" (in some cases removing a block the Pokémon dislikes didn't raise comfort). So a disliked block holds comfort down, and removing it is meant to raise it again — [Game8 JP update log](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/771132)
- **[Guide, Game8 JP 769142, 2026-04-30]** Dropping a house below 3 furniture during rearranging makes it stop counting as a house, and its residents lose their home (back to すみかなし) — [Game8 JP 模様替え](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/769142)

### Inferences
- For the planner, a layout sets a **ceiling** that the Pokémon reaches only through events: noticing each item, being fed, and being asked. The fastest route seen (homeless to 最高 in one day) needs every factor in place before move-in, favourite food given, and the Pokémon walked past its items. A planner can promise "最高 reachable in 1 day if fed and walked through"; a wish-only route caps the climb at one rank a day (up to 5 days from びみょう).
- Falls appear tied to discrete events (home lost, a liked item removed, a fire put out, a disliked item added) rather than daily decay. The safest wording for the app is that comfort is unlikely to fall while the layout is unchanged, and that re-homing a Pokémon resets it to the new home's starting rank.
- ぼってぃ's failed attempts to lower rank by environment changes suggest environment acts more as a cap on gains than as a penalty on an existing rank, but this rests on one tester and is not proven.

### Gaps
- No test measures how many ranks are lost when a liked item or the favourite environment is removed, or how quickly (same day, next day, on noticing).
- The "past best" ceiling (up to 3 days to exceed) and any hidden accumulated score remain unverified.
- No test confirms whether minigames, requests or Scrub cleaning each give a separate rank or share a daily cap with the wish.
- Whether comfort rises with nothing changed and no interaction (pure time) is unanswered; the only "time" effects seen are delayed noticing (ぼってぃ, ぽこポケ学会).

## 3. Food: delivery, hunger, flavour effects and the flavour table

### Takeaway
Food raises comfort and is the wish a layout can't pre-empt. It can be handed over directly or left on a plate, which Pokémon "may" come and eat; nothing found shows trees feeding residents. The food contribution has a **per-Pokémon cap**: a favourite-flavour cooked dish counts most, a non-favourite raw ingredient only slightly, and seven days of favourite berries alone stopped at ちょっといい感じ. No source says how long "fed" lasts.

### Cited Findings
- **[Official item text, via GameWith]** Wooden plate (木のおさら) description: 「お皿に食べ物をのせておくとポケモンたちが食べにくるかも。」 (Put food on the plate and Pokémon may come to eat it.) — [GameWith 547774](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/547774)
- **[Guide, Game8 JP 767513]** "食べ物は直接あげることもできる他、お皿などの上に置いてあげることもできます" (food can be handed directly or placed on a plate or similar) — [Game8 JP 767513](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767513)
- **[Guide, Nintendo Life, 2026-07-29]** Iffy Pokémon "are mainly hungry and could do with some food … You can either put it on a dish in their habitat or you can give it to them directly. Either way is good. Do this whether they ask for it or not" — [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/pokemon-pokopia-how-to-raise-comfort-levels)
- **[Guide, search summary]** Bulbapedia: when a Pokémon is hungry, it will eat on its own (stub article) — [Bulbapedia: Comfort level](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Comfort_level) (search summary)
- **[Guide, GameWith 545675, updated 2026-11-26]** Give food with 「これ見て！」; "ポケモンがお腹いっぱいになったり、好みの食べ物を食べたりすることですみごこちが良くなります" (being full, or eating a liked food, improves comfort) — [GameWith 545675](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/545675)
- **[Community-tested, ぽこポケ学会]** Not being hungry is the third most important factor for 最高, after no disliked environment and a met liked environment — [ぽこポケ学会](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/)
- **[Community-tested, ぼってぃ]** The tester scores food 0 (none), 1 (food) or 2 (favourite flavour), probably needed for 最高; notes "好きな「食事」をあげている（永続？）" (favourite food given, permanent?); a plain berry given first may use up "当日の食事枠" (the day's meal slot) so a favourite given after it doesn't count; favourite sour Aspear (ナナシのみ) to Machoke for 7 days never got past ちょっといい感じ. Open questions the tester lists: whether a Pokémon given no food at all can reach 最高, and "バスケットからの食事供給ですみごこちランクがあがるのか" (whether food supplied from a basket raises rank) — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Guide, 攻略大百科 2474, updated 2026-11-16]** "食べ物をあげてすみごこちを上げるのは、ポケモンごとに上限があります。すみごこちが最も上げられるのは「好物の料理」で、わずかに上げられるのは「好物ではない食材」です。" (the comfort food can add has a per-Pokémon cap; a favourite dish raises it most, a non-favourite ingredient only slightly) — [攻略大百科 2474](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/2474)
- **[Guide, 攻略大百科 3509]** For a food wish, cooked dishes are fine but raw ingredients are recommended because they take no effort ("料理を与えても構いませんが、食材は手間がかからないのでオススメ"). Raw foods by flavour: あまい マメ, モモンのみ; しぶい カゴのみ, コムギ, たべごろキノコ; すっぱい ナナシのみ, トマト; にがい かいそう, ジャガイモ, チーゴのみ; からい たべごろニンジン; 普通 ヒメリのみ, ラムのみ — [攻略大百科 3509](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/3509)

Bubbly Basin additions: ぷちぷちサラダ bitter, ぷちぷちスープ bitter, スイカパン sweet, はじけるハンバーグ spicy, スイカスムージー sweet, うみぶどうスムージー dry, ヒメリスムージー sweet, レッドホットスムージー spicy, さっぱりソーダスムージー sour, コーヒーパフェスムージー bitter. The planner should mark food as "player action", and may suggest a plate with the favourite flavour in range as a convenience, not a guarantee.
- Since the food contribution is capped and favourite food alone stopped at ちょっといい感じ, food cannot replace rest, toy, decoration and environment in the layout ceiling.
- For the app's flavour lookup, raw favourites that need no cooking exist for every flavour except a spicy berry and a bitter drink (spicy: たべごろニンジン; bitter: チーゴ / ジャガイモ / かいそう).

### Gaps
- When a fed Pokémon gets hungry again, and whether being fed lasts until 05:00, is not stated anywhere found.
- Whether the favourite-food effect on comfort persists after that day (ぼってぃ's "永続？") is untested.
- Whether plate or basket food, or berries on trees in range, count as "fed" or "favourite given" is untested.
- How much a dish beats a raw ingredient for comfort, beyond 攻略大百科's ordering, is not measured.

## 4. Friendship and its interaction with comfort

### Takeaway
Friendship is a separate hidden meter that has never been seen to fall. Most actions that raise it (gifts, minigames, 「・・・」 talks) also nudge comfort; the guide's team confirms a comfort gain separate from the wish, but one tester saw no rank change from a 「・・・」 talk. Comfort affects friendship speed (lower comfort needs more gifts), not the other way round, and no source gives best friends a comfort effect.

### Cited Findings
- **[Guide, Serebii]** Friendship "is different to the Comfort Level that they have, though their friendship can affect the growth of that as well". At maximum the Pokémon announces it is your Best Friend, is always happy to see you, and gets a Pokédex mark. Look That Way: playing always boosts friendship, winning boosts it more — [Serebii Friendship](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/friendship.shtml)
- **[Guide, 攻略大百科, 2026-10-28]** Friendship (仲良し度) "環境レベルや「すみごこち」とは別にある要素" (separate from environment level and comfort). Signs: you're called by name instead of メタモン, it gives presents, special talks, no heart once maxed, a Pokédex heart at max. Not seen to fall after long neglect. Comfort, unlike friendship, can fall from furniture, toys or food it doesn't like. Benefits include "すみごこちも同時にアップする" (comfort rises too): friendship actions raise comfort apart from the wish (confirmed). Gifts raise friendship once per day; play invitations and 「・・・」 talks add more; game outcome doesn't matter — [攻略大百科 4899](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/4899)
- **[Community-tested by the guide's team, 攻略大百科 4899]** Favourite-flavour berries to max friendship, by comfort: びみょう or すみかなし **8**; just appeared from a new habitat **7**; ちょっといい感じ **6**; ふつう after many days **6 + one Look This Way**. "すみごこちが良くないポケモンは、仲良し度が最大になるまでに回数が増える傾向" (low-comfort Pokémon tend to need more) — [攻略大百科 4899](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/4899)
- **[Guide, Game8 EN Look That Way, 2026-03-08]** "Victory will significantly improve your friendship … even when you lose, your friendship still improves to some extent"; this contradicts 攻略大百科's "勝敗は影響しない" — [Game8 EN mini games](https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Pokopia/archives/585464); contradicted by [攻略大百科 4899](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/4899)
- **[Community-tested, ぼってぃ, Mar 2026]** A 「・・・」 talk showed a heart (friendship) but left comfort at ふつう; the tester adds friendship ("好感度") as a further unknown on top of their comfort model — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Guide, Nintendo Life]** "You'll also know your friend is satisfied if they start referring to you by your character's name" (this mixes friendship signs into comfort) — [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/pokemon-pokopia-how-to-raise-comfort-levels)
- **[Official, Ver. 1.1.0]** More conversation variants for Pokémon with a 「・・・」 bubble, and the interval and topic bias of those talks adjusted — [Game8 JP update log](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/771132). **[Official, Ver. 2.0.0]** Ordinary conversation indicators reduced so special talks and requests stand out — [Serebii patches](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/patch.shtml)

### Inferences
- The planner can leave friendship out of the layout ceiling. In the app it belongs as a "player actions that also nudge comfort" note (daily gift, accept games, talk to 「・・・」).
- "Lower comfort needs more gifts" means good layouts also speed up friendship: a side benefit to mention, not to compute.

### Gaps
- No source quantifies how much comfort a friendship action gives, whether it can lift a rank on its own, or whether it shares the one-a-day cap.
- No source says best-friend status changes comfort gains or gives a floor.

## 5. Patch history touching comfort, wishes, requests, noticing, furniture removal and the PC screen

### Takeaway
Only three patches touch comfort. Ver. 1.1.0 added the daily-wish checkmark, retuned two items' comfort settings and fixed disliked-block removal and false brightness talk. Ver. 2.0.0 made Pokémon re-notice replaced furniture faster, let them move back into a rebuilt habitat, stopped requests asking for disliked items and added Scrub cleaning. Ver. 2.0.1 and the 1.0.x patches changed nothing in comfort. Serebii's English list leaves out several of the 1.1.0 comfort lines that the Japanese notes include.

### Cited Findings
- **[Official, Ver. 1.0.1, 4 Mar 2026]** Online features and bug fixes only — [Serebii patches](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/patch.shtml)
- **[Official, Ver. 1.0.2, 18 Mar per Game8 JP (Serebii dates it 4 Mar)]** Story-request fixes (Squirtle in a tree, Tangrowth bridge, Rotom, Spinarak type); nothing on comfort — [Game8 JP update log](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/771132); [Serebii patches](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/patch.shtml)
- **[Official, Ver. 1.0.3, 9 Apr]** Improved: homeless Pokémon (すみかがなくなってしまったポケモン) could not be found with the Pokédex search. - **[Official, Ver. 1.0.4, 23 Apr]** Pokémon Centers movable during events; some Pokémon failed to appear while others were waiting; nothing on comfort — [Serebii patches](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/patch.shtml)
- **[Official, Ver. 1.1.0, 10 Jun JP]** Improvements: "「環境レベル」の詳細画面で、その日の「すみごこち」のお願いを叶えたポケモンが分かるようにしました" (✅ per 攻略大百科); "家具「ふろばのいす」「いしのだんろ」の「すみごこち」に対する設定を、より自然なものに修正しました" (comfort settings of the bath chair and stone fireplace made more natural); 「・・・」 talk variants, interval and topic bias; companions share chairs and beds; floor choice when moving into two-storey houses; "家を建てる" requests made clearer; multiplayer asks whether to take a Pokémon's request. - **[Official, Ver. 1.1.1, 19 Jun]** Problems when too many items are placed; photo-mode expression fix — [Serebii patches](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/patch.shtml)
- **[Official, Ver. 2.0.0, 5 Aug]** Improvements: "ポケモンの「すみか」を壊してしまっても、一定範囲内に同じものを作り直せば、再度住み直してくれるようにしました" (a Pokémon moves back if the same habitat is rebuilt within a certain range); "家具がなくなって悲しんでいるポケモンが、同じ家具を置き直した時に、より気づきやすくしました"; ordinary talk indicators toned down. Fixes: requests could ask for items the requester dislikes; honey sometimes failed to bring Pokémon home; Magnet Rise made Pokémon scold the player; water-averse Pokémon couldn't cross duckweed. ### Inferences
- Tests dated before 10 Jun 2026 (ぼってぃ on 16 Mar, Game8 JP 767513 on 31 Mar) predate the 1.1.0 fixes to disliked-block removal and false brightness talk, so a "removing it didn't help" result from that period may have been the bug.
- The 1.1.0 fix "liked brightness should extend activity time" shows brightness affects behaviour (time out of the home), not only comfort.
- Two patches tie Pokémon state to "noticing" (2.0.0 furniture re-noticing) and to a range check on rebuilding (2.0.0 "一定範囲内"), which supports modelling comfort as event-driven re-scoring.

### Gaps
- The official notes don't say what the bath chair and stone fireplace settings were before or after (likely tag or size changes); no source explains it.
- The radius of "一定範囲内" (a certain range) for moving back into a rebuilt habitat is not given.
- No patch mentions wish generation, the past-best ceiling, or the daily wish count.

## 6. What the PC comfort screen shows, and whether Pokémon you can't see still change

### Takeaway
The PC's − button opens the Environment Level details: a per-Pokémon list of current rank names for **this area only**, plus since 1.1.0 a ✅ beside Pokémon whose wish was granted today. No source shows a meter, score or hint there; the hint comes only from asking the Pokémon. Pokémon that aren't rendered don't notice changes, so their comfort lags until they are seen or the area reloads. Nothing found says whether comfort changes in towns you aren't in.

### Cited Findings
- **[Guide, Game8 JP 767513; GameWith 545675]** Open the PC and press − to list every Pokémon's current comfort; asking 「すみごこち どう？」 shows one Pokémon's comfort and its daily wish — [Game8 JP 767513](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767513); [GameWith 545675](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/545675)
- **[Guide, Game8 EN, 2026-03-05]** The list is the "Environment Level details screen, which can be accessed from the Pokemon Center PC"; asking gives "hints on what you can do to improve their habitat" — [Game8 EN comfort](https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Pokopia/archives/583142)
- **[Guide, 攻略大百科 3509]** "自分がいるエリアのポケモンの評価のみを確認できる" (only the current area's Pokémon); to see another area, go there — [攻略大百科 3509](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/3509)
- **[Official, Ver. 1.1.0]** The details screen marks Pokémon whose comfort wish was granted that day (✅) — [Game8 JP update log](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/771132); [攻略大百科 Ver.1.1.0](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/5597)
- **[Guide, Nintendo Life]** Rank meanings as described: Awesome highest; Great "could do with a little something extra"; Nice "one or two nice conveniences ticked off"; Average "usually what your Pokémon will start out as — they've just moved in"; Iffy "despite making a habitat a 'mon likes … Perhaps it needs a cave, or some light, or a bit of food?"; No Home "never had a home or … displaced after you've changed things around" — [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/pokemon-pokopia-how-to-raise-comfort-levels)
- **[Community-tested, ぼってぃ]** Not rendered means not noticed. Some rises happen "何もしていないタイミング" (when you did nothing), explained as noticing older placements or Pokémon drawn in from out of view. A destroyed house reads すみかなし on the PC at once, while the level waits until the Pokémon notices or a load happens. When the total drops across a level boundary the level is held with slack (e.g. Lv5 kept down to about 50 points after falling below 52) — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Guide, 攻略大百科 3417, from the earlier notes]** About 25 Pokémon appear per area at once; Pokémon in kit houses with a loading screen don't count; above 25, homeless Pokémon may not appear — [攻略大百科 3417](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/3417)
- **[Community-tested, ぽこポケ学会]** A Pokémon with no wish often turns 最高 after time passes or an area change, consistent with re-scoring on load — [ぽこポケ学会](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/)

### Inferences
- The planner's per-Pokémon ceiling has no in-game number to check against; only the rank name and the daily ✅ can be read off the PC. The app's best check is "the predicted ceiling matches the PC rank after the Pokémon has seen its home and been fed".
- Residents beyond the ~25 rendered, or in kit-house interiors you never enter, may sit below their ceiling until you visit or reload; that is a gap between layout and in-game rank the app can warn about.

### Gaps
- No source describes any meter, numeric score or factor breakdown on the PC screen beyond rank names (and the 1.1.0 ✅); no screenshot transcription was found.
- Whether comfort changes for Pokémon in a town you are not in (e.g. rain putting out a fire there) is unanswered.
- Whether Pokémon that aren't displayed (over the ~25 cap) can still notice changes or get fed by plates is unanswered.
