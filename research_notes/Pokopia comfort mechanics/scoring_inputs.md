# Pokopia comfort (すみごこち): positive inputs and how they score

Researched 2026-11-26 against Ver. 2.0.1 (released 2026-09-08). Labels on every finding: **official** (Nintendo patch text or in-game text), **community-tested** (a player measured it; version given), **guide** (a guide site states it without a test), **unverified** (one anecdote, a fan tool with no test, or a page that looks generated), and **search summary** (the page was blocked, so only search-result text was read). Pages were read with curl through the proxy. Altema, gameranbu, lifematome, Yahoo!知恵袋, pokopiaguide, gamertw, pokopiacompanion and arahog-jp.github.io were refused by the proxy. GameFAQs and thegameswiki answered with bot-check pages. Facts from those come from search summaries only. No datamine of the comfort formula was found anywhere.

Test dates and game versions: ぼってぃ's note was published 2026-03-16 (Ver. 1.0.1) and never edited ([note API](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)). ぽこポケ学会's house page was last changed 2026-05-17 (Ver. 1.0.4), per the repo's commit log for docs/house.md ([kyu49/pokopia](https://github.com/kyu49/pokopia/commits/main/docs/house.md)). がんも's note dates from 2026-03-26 (Ver. 1.0.2) and ろぜ's from 2026-05-12 (Ver. 1.0.4). **No comfort test newer than Ver. 1.1.0 (2026-06-09) was found.**

## 1. Do favourites count only through rest, toy and decoration items, or does any favourite item in range help? How many favourite categories must be present?

### Takeaway
No test settles this. ぼってぃ's model counts favourites only as a bonus on rest, toy and decoration items. ぽこポケ学会 lists "some favourites in range" as a factor of its own. Serebii says any item matching a favourite raises comfort faster. In-game wishes name a favourite and a tag together (for example "shiny" + rest), which fits ぼってぃ's per-slot reading best. No source gives a number of the 5 favourite categories that must be present.

### Cited Findings
- **[Guide, Serebii]** "Every item in the game fits in to several of these categories… If you also decorate their home or habitat with these items, then they will also have their Comfy Level increase at a higher rate. You will notice this as it puts down a bigger sparkle when you put it down on the floor in their home." It does not limit this to tagged items. — [Serebii: Favorites](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/favorites.shtml)
- **[Community-tested, ぽこポケ学会, Ver. 1.0.x]** Factors for 最高, roughly most important first: (1) no disliked environment within range; (2) favourite environment met; (3) not hungry; (4) 「きゅうけい/おもちゃ/かざりつけの3つが全て認識可能範囲にある」 (a rest item, a toy and a decoration all within range); (5) 「好きなものの内いくつかが認識可能範囲にある(同じ家具は加算されない or 効果が著しく薄れる？)」 (some of its favourites within range; the same furniture doesn't add, or adds much less?). Favourites are a **separate line from the trio**, and the count is only "いくつか" (some). The page opens with 「未解明な部分が多い」 (much is unexplained). — [ぽこポケ学会: 家と住み心地](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/)
- **[Community-tested hypothesis, ぼってぃ, Ver. 1.0.1]** Favourites enter only as 2 instead of 1 on each rest, toy or decoration factor. The worked example (Lotad line) scores 「休憩:自然を感じる2pt」 (a rest item with the Lots of nature favourite), 「おもちゃ:音が鳴る2pt」 (Noisy stuff toy) and 「かざりつけ:いれもの2pt」 (Containers decoration). In every 最高 case 「好きな「おもちゃ」「かざりつけ」を3種満たしている」 (three kinds of favourite toys or decorations were in place). The tester also says 「好物判定が、すみごこちチェックでしか確定できない」 (only the comfort check confirms a favourite match) and 「重複するかも謎なので、とりあえず多めに配置しておくが最適解」 (it's unclear whether they overlap, so the best answer is to place plenty). — [ぼってぃ: 環境レベルを検証する](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Community-tested, ぼってぃ]** 「好きなアイテム以外を置き続けても「ちょっといい感じ」以上にはならない」 (placing only non-favourite items never got past Nice). 「「すみごこちはどう？」で出てくるアイテム種でなくても、ポケモンが観測すればすみごこちは上がった」 (items other than the one the wish named still raised comfort once the Pokémon noticed them). — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Unverified anecdote, ろぜ, Ver. 1.0.4]** Wishes name a favourite and a tag together: 「ドンカラスがキラキラの休憩を要求してきたのでゲーミングベッドを置いたら、まぶしいな…と言われました。ゴージャスな家具でダメならそれしかない」 (Honchkrow asked for a *shiny rest item*. Luxury furniture didn't satisfy it, and a gaming bed did but made it complain about brightness). It reached 最高 once afterwards. Most of this player's residents reached 最高 right after moving in because 「好みの環境と家具を揃えていた」 (favourite environment and furniture were already in place), followed by food. — [ろぜ: 感想 ③満足編](https://note.com/roze_flare999/n/nc89b8a22469e)
- **[Guide, 攻略大百科, environment-level page]** 「光る床を壊すことで入手できる「おおきなおとしもの」は、設置することで喜んでもらえる確率もあがる」 (placing the large lost relic from glowing ground raises the chance a Pokémon is pleased). Serebii lists the Large Lost Relic as Category **Other**, **no tag**, favourite **Strange stuff**. This is the only claim found that an untagged, favourite-bearing item helps. Another example on the same page: a Pokémon that likes 「水を感じる」 (Lots of water) is pleased by a shower, which Serebii tags as a Toy. — [攻略大百科: 環境レベル上げ](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/1760); [Serebii: Large Lost Relic](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items/largelostrelic.shtml); [Serebii: Toy items](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items/toy.shtml)
- **[Guide, GameWith, updated 2026-11-26]** 「好みに合うものをすみかの近くに置くことで、すみごこちがぐっと良くなります」 (placing things that match its tastes near its home raises comfort a lot). Separately it says to prioritise rest, play and decoration items. — [GameWith: すみごこち](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/545675)
- **[Guide, Game8 JP]** Comfort also rises from things beyond the daily wish: 「ポケモン図鑑の「好きな環境」「好きなもの」などを参考に、様々な方法を試してみましょう」 (use the Pokédex's favourite environment and favourites to try various things). — [Game8 JP: すみごこち](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767513)
- **[Unverified anecdote, がんも, Ver. 1.0.2]** A punching bag in a block house sharply raised the comfort of Scyther, Timburr and Hitmonchan, all of which like 「トレーニングできる」 (Exercise). Wooden furniture plus dolls helped Bulbasaur. うすチュウ's comfort 「露骨に下がる」 (dropped visibly) with a metal drum in its home (a Bleak Beach story resident; English name not checked), which the player put down to its liking 「せいけつ」 (Cleanliness). The punching bag is a Toy. Serebii gives the metal drum the Decoration tag and Construction / Containers / Metal stuff / Round stuff favourites, so no listed dislike explains that drop. — [がんも: 最小催行人数クリア](https://note.com/ganmo_umai/n/nc20c24282d4d); [Serebii: Metal drum](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items/metaldrum.shtml)
- **[Unverified, fan tool, search summary]** 「ぽこあ施設診断」 weights favourites by their order on the Pokédex screen: 5/4/3/2/1 points from the top. Only the best match per item counts, and an item with a disliked attribute scores 0. These scores feed a facility "match %", not comfort, and no test is cited. — [ぽこあ施設診断](https://arahog-jp.github.io/) (search summary)
- **[Unverified, fan tool]** PokoTraqr: "The more tags you match, the higher their comfort tier — from Iffy (0-24%) to Awesome (90%+)". No test or source is given. — [PokoTraqr Housing Guide](https://www.pokotraqr.com/guides/housing-guide)

### Inferences
- Wishes such as "shiny rest item", "nature rest item" and "noisy toy" pair one favourite with one tag. That suggests the game scores favourites through the tagged slots, which is ぼってぃ's model and the app's current rule. ぽこポケ学会's separate line (5) may describe the same thing seen from the item side rather than an independent bonus. The two cannot be told apart from the published tests.
- The only untagged items that carry favourites are tables, desks, counters, storage boxes, safes, treasure chests, pedestals, some building parts (doors, windows, ladders, chains), some blocks (Stone brick wall: Hard stuff, Stone stuff) and flowers (Pretty flowers). Rugs, posters, block prints and wallpapers carry none on Serebii (see section 4). If the game did count untagged favourites, the effect would come mostly from tables and storage. The 攻略大百科 relic claim is the only pointer that it does.
- App check: "favourite items that aren't rest/toy/decoration add nothing" is consistent with ぼってぃ and with the wish format, but it is unproven. A safe option is to show untagged favourites as "may help" without adding points.
- App check: nothing supports a requirement for a set number of the 5 favourite categories. ぼってぃ's "three kinds of favourite toys or decorations" in every 最高 case is the nearest evidence, and the app's 3-item toy/decoration slot already reflects it.

### Gaps
- No test places an untagged favourite item (a table or storage box) alone and records a rank change. ぼってぃ notes that favourite matches can only be confirmed through the comfort check.
- No source says whether matching more than one favourite category on a single item (most items have 2–5) scores more than matching one.
- How many of a Pokémon's favourite categories must be present: no source.

## 2. How many toys, decorations and rest items count? Does a second rest item help? Is there a cap?

### Takeaway
The only measured cap is for Ditto's own house: comfort maxes at 13 kinds, with at most 5 kinds each of toy, rest and decoration evaluated. For residents, ぼってぃ's "3 toys or decorations in total, at least one of each, one rest item" is inferred from observed 最高 patterns, not measured. No source tests a second rest item. Caps such as "6 seats" or "1 bed" come from a page that invents numbers.

### Cited Findings
- **[Community-tested hypothesis, ぼってぃ, Ver. 1.0.1]** First version: rest 0–2 (「必須？」, required?), toys 0–4 (「最大2個？」, up to 2?), decorations 0–4 (up to 2?), food 0–2, environment 0 or 2. Later version: 「おもちゃ、かざりつけ加点：0～6」 (toy + decoration 0–6), because 「おもちゃ3種やかざりつけ3種がいまのところ発生していないことから、おもちゃ、かざりつけは確定で1種ずつ、3種目がおもちゃなのか、かざりつけなのかは、個体差あり？」 (three toys or three decorations have never occurred so far, so one toy and one decoration seem fixed, and whether the third is a toy or a decoration may differ per Pokémon). Rest stays a single 0–2 factor. — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Community-tested, ぽこポケ学会, Ver. 1.0.x]** Ditto's house: 「13種類で最大。おもちゃ・きゅうけい・かざりつけはそれぞれ5種類までしか評価されない。環境レベルに影響する。」 (maxes at 13 kinds; toy, rest and decoration are each evaluated only up to 5 kinds; it affects the Environment Level). The page also says that with enough furniture, Ditto's comfort can reach 最高. — [ぽこポケ学会](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/)
- **[Community-tested, ぽこポケ学会]** For residents, the threshold condition is that "the 3" (rest, toy and decoration) are all within range. No count beyond one of each is given. — [ぽこポケ学会](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/)
- **[Guide, 攻略大百科]** "ポケモンは、自身より小さいベッドやイスを使うことはできません。大きめのベッドやイスがオススメです" (Pokémon can't use a bed or chair smaller than themselves; bigger ones are recommended). Altema says the same, and that furniture outside the range has no effect (search summary). A 知恵袋 question asks whether size-mismatched favourites have no effect (search summary, unanswered). — [攻略大百科: すみごこち](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/3509); [Altema](https://altema.jp/pocoapokemon/sumigogoti) (search summary); [Yahoo!知恵袋](https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q12326714292) (search summary)
- **[Official, Ver. 1.1.0 patch]** "Pokémon accompanying Ditto can now use chairs and beds together where possible." This is about sharing a seat, not about comfort counts. — [Serebii: Patches](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/patch.shtml)
- **[Unverified, likely generated]** pokopia.center's furniture-categories page says "comfort caps at 6 seating pieces per room", "Beds also cap at 1 per room", there is a "+5 comfort bonus" for 3+ pieces of one set, and a "90 comfort" target for Rayquaza. None of these sets, numbers or targets appear in any other source. The same site's comfort guide says it "previously published an invented four-level system… Those claims have been removed." — [pokopia.center: furniture categories](https://pokopia.center/posts/pokopia-furniture-categories-complete-guide-2026/); [pokopia.center: comfort guide](https://pokopia.center/posts/pokopia-comfort-level-furniture-guide-2026/)
- **[Guide, search summary]** RankedBoost says each crafted furniture piece adds comfort points and better furniture gives more. No test is cited. — [RankedBoost](https://rankedboost.com/pokopia/guides/how-to-raise-comfort-level/) (search summary)

### Inferences
- Ditto's cap is per kind and per tag (5 + 5 + 5, total 13). That shows the game evaluates **kinds within each tag**, and it makes a per-tag count for residents plausible. The resident caps may be larger than ぼってぃ's 1 rest + 3 toys/decorations, which comes from the 最高 cases seen rather than from reaching a ceiling.
- App check: "1 rest item" and "3 different toys/decorations, at most 2 of either" match ぼってぃ's later variant. They are a reasonable working model but not a measured cap. Because 9+ already gives Awesome, a larger true cap would rarely change the predicted rank. It would mostly let a Pokémon with an unmet environment reach Awesome without favourites.
- A second rest item probably adds nothing under ぼってぃ's model. Nothing contradicts that, and nothing confirms it either.

### Gaps
- No test of a second rest item, a fourth toy or decoration, or three toys and no decoration.
- No test of whether a rest item the Pokémon doesn't fit on scores 0 or 1. The app's "it must fit" rule rests on 攻略大百科's "can't use" statement only.

## 3. Do duplicate copies count? Do colour variants or "the same furniture" count as different?

### Takeaway
Every source that counts furniture counts *kinds* (種類), and the game itself tracks "the same furniture". Copies of one item almost certainly add nothing, or very little. No source tests repainted copies. Different items in the same series (a plain chair and a plain sofa) are different kinds.

### Cited Findings
- **[Community-tested, ぽこポケ学会]** 「同じ家具は加算されない or 効果が著しく薄れる？」 (the same furniture doesn't add, or its effect is much weaker?). It is marked with a question mark. — [ぽこポケ学会](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/)
- **[Community-tested, ぽこポケ学会]** Ditto's house is evaluated in 種類 (kinds): 13 kinds maximum, 5 kinds per tag. — [ぽこポケ学会](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/)
- **[Guide]** The move-in check needs **three different** pieces: GameWith says 「異なる家具を設置する必要がある」 (you need different furniture), 攻略大百科 says 「家具が3種類必要」 (three kinds needed), and Serebii says "Pokémon will not move in unless there's 3 unique pieces of furniture". — [GameWith](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/545675); [攻略大百科](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/3509); [Serebii: Building](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/building.shtml)
- **[Official, Ver. 2.0.0, Japanese text via 攻略大百科]** 「家具がなくなって悲しんでいるポケモンが、同じ家具を置き直した時に、より気づきやすくしました」 (a Pokémon saddened by removed furniture now notices sooner when the same furniture is put back). The game tracks individual furniture identity, and removing furniture lowers comfort. — [攻略大百科: Ver.2.0.0](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/5548); [Serebii: Patches](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/patch.shtml)
- **[Guide, search summary]** gameranbu: 「同じ家具を大量に置くよりも、違う種類の家具を組み合わせて配置したほうが、総合的な環境値が伸びやすくなります」 (combining different kinds raises comfort more than placing many of the same item). — [gameranbu](https://gameranbu.jp/pocoapokemon/b4d5600e91087e3eba87) (search summary)
- **[Community-tested, ぼってぃ]** Whether favourites stack is 「謎」 (a mystery). — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Guide, Serebii]** "Many of the furniture can also have their colourings changed by Smeargle, either once or in two different parts." Ver. 2.0.0 made more items paintable. Painting changes an existing item's colours and does not create a different item. — [Serebii: Furniture](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/furniture.shtml); [Serebii: Patches](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/patch.shtml)

### Inferences
- App check: "copies of one item count once" is the best-supported rule. Painted copies should also count once, since painting is a colour state of the same item. That is an inference with no test behind it.
- Items that share a style name but are different objects (Antique chair, Antique sofa, Antique bed) are separate kinds for the move-in check, so they should count separately for comfort too.

### Gaps
- No test of whether a second copy adds "much less" (ぽこポケ学会's alternative) or nothing at all.
- No test of painted variants.

## 4. Which items carry a tag? Do untagged items count? Does placement matter (wall, table top, outside the walls, out of reach)?

### Takeaway
Serebii lists 79 Relaxation, 148 Toy and 229 Decoration items, and no item has two tags. Tables, desks, counters, storage boxes and safes have no tag. Posters, rugs, block prints and wallpapers have neither a tag nor any favourite category. Wall-hung toys and decorations count. Furniture outside a block house's walls counts if it is inside the dotted range and the Pokémon can reach it. Light and climate items count only inside the house or directly above or below the door. No source covers items stacked on tables.

### Cited Findings
- **[Guide, Serebii item lists]** Relaxation: 79 items, including every stool, chair, bench, sofa and bed, plus **Campfire, Torch, Stone fireplace, Perch, Inflatable boat, Beach parasol, Sleeping bag and swim rings**. Toy: 148, including mirrors, dressers, books, papers, sinks, fridge, washing machine, shower, tablet, PCs, TV, arcade machine, dolls, music mats, Horsea and Gyarados fountains, pumpkin lanterns and Trevenant streetlight. Decoration: 229, including **storage** (chests, closets, shelves, bookcases, wall storage box), **food props** (mug, tea set, pizza, cakes), potted plants, **all lamps and most streetlights**, fossils, signs, bathtub, toilet, cooking stove, air conditioner, humidifier, bonfire, firepit and candles. My parse of the three lists found no item in more than one. Each list page says its items "can be used to boost a Pokémon's comfy level when they ask for an item of this category". — [Serebii: Relaxation](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items/relaxation.shtml); [Toy](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items/toy.shtml); [Decoration](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items/decoration.shtml)
- **[Guide, Serebii furniture page]** "Some of the furniture has the Relaxation and Decoration flags which some Pokémon may request to increase their Comfy Level while others are mostly there for utility and to look good." 40 of the page's 141 furniture items have no tag: every table and desk, counters, storage box and big storage box, safe, treasure chests, pedestal, push cart, iron rack and exhibition stand. — [Serebii: Furniture](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/furniture.shtml)
- **[Guide, Serebii item pages]** Untagged items can still carry favourites: Plain table (Furniture, no tag, Wooden stuff); Antique table (Luxury, Wooden stuff); Stone brick wall (Blocks, no tag, Hard stuff, Stone stuff); Wildflowers (Nature, no tag, Pretty flowers); Large Lost Relic (Other, no tag, Strange stuff). Others carry **no favourite at all**: Acrylic poster (Other, no tag, none); Ocean pals rug (Other, no tag, none); Argyle print block (Blocks, no tag, none). Serebii's favourite-category pages list tables under Luxury and Colorful stuff but no rug or poster. — [Plain table](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items/plaintable.shtml); [Antique table](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items/antiquetable.shtml); [Stone brick wall](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items/stonebrickwall.shtml); [Wildflowers](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items/wildflowers.shtml); [Acrylic poster](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items/acrylicposter.shtml); [Ocean pals rug](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items/oceanpalsrug.shtml); [Argyle print](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items/argyleprint.shtml); [Serebii: Luxury favourites](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/favorites/luxury.shtml)
- **[Guide, Serebii]** Tags can sit alongside environment roles: Campfire is Relaxation with the Fire flag (Group Activities, Lots of fire, Stone stuff). Mushroom lamp is a Decoration (Electronics, Round stuff, Wooden stuff) that "emits a gentle glow". — [Serebii: Campfire](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items/campfire.shtml); [Serebii: Mushroom lamp](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items/mushroomlamp.shtml)
- **[Guide, GameWith]** Wallpaper (e.g. ぬののカベ, フローリングのゆか) has 分類 (class) 「その他」 (other). It can only be applied inside large kit houses, not block houses. — [GameWith: ぬののカベ](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/549674)
- **[Guide, Game8 JP]** Toys and decorations both include 「壁にかけるものや床に置く物」 (wall-hung and floor items). Rest items are 「イスやベッドなどの「きゅうけい」に分類されるアイテム」 (items classed as rest, such as chairs and beds). — [Game8 JP: すみごこち](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767513)
- **[Community-tested, ぽこポケ学会, Ver. 1.0.x]** Range rules: 「家と見なされるための条件用の家具とは別に、周囲にある家具も住心地に影響を与える」 (besides the three furniture pieces that make it a house, furniture around it also affects comfort). The range shows as a white dotted line when you press R with a resident present. 「ポケモンがアクセス不可能だと、住心地に影響を与えない」 (if the Pokémon can't reach it, it has no effect). If the door is at or below the 5th block above ground, items on the ground are recognised, and items up to 3 blocks above the door's height are too, giving a door-relative band of **−4 to +3**. Furniture can be shared with another house, including furniture inside a house. **Light sources and other environment items** use a different range: inside the house and directly above or below the door only, floor −1 to +3 (marked 検証不十分, not fully tested; the space in front of the door probably counts too). — [ぽこポケ学会](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/)
- **[Guide, Game8 JP, updated 2026-11-26]** Overlapping ranges: an item in the overlap counts for both homes. 「家に住んでいるポケモンのすみごこちは、家の外の環境を整えても変化しなくなります。家に住まわせた後は、家具やかざりつけを置いて、家の中の環境を整えてあげましょう」 (a resident's comfort no longer changes when you improve the outdoor environment, so furnish the inside). — [Game8 JP: 引っ越し](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767983)
- **[Guide, 攻略大百科]** Kit houses (おへや etc.) have a 「完全に独立した一室」 (a fully separate room). Tags are shown in the item details. — [攻略大百科: 家の判定](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/1611); [攻略大百科: すみごこち](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/3509)
- **[Guide, Serebii flavour text]** Tables invite stacking ("Try putting different things on it"), and several Luxury decorations "would look great on top of a table". Nothing says whether stacked items count. — [Serebii: Plain table](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items/plaintable.shtml); [Serebii: Luxury favourites](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/favorites/luxury.shtml)

### Inferences
- App check: tables, storage boxes, counters, desks and safes are the main untagged items that carry favourites. Posters, rugs, prints and wallpaper have no tag and no favourite, so they can't add comfort under any reading.
- Campfire, torch and stone fireplace are rest items. A planner that suggests a rest item for a Grass type must avoid them, since fire is a Grass dislike.
- Lamps are Decorations. For a dark-loving Pokémon a lamp fills a decoration slot but can break the Dark environment (ろぜ's Honchkrow "too bright"). This needs a warning, not just points.
- For a block house, items outside the walls but inside the dotted range count for furniture (ぽこポケ学会). Game8's "outdoor environment doesn't matter" is about light, warmth and humidity, which ぽこポケ学会 also limits to inside and above or below the door. For a kit house, the interior is a separate map, so outside items presumably don't reach the residents (inference).
- Stacked items probably count if they are inside the vertical band and reachable. That is untested.

### Gaps
- No test of items placed on tables or shelves, or of wall-hung items above +3.
- Serebii's favourite data for rugs and posters may be incomplete, since its own text says every item has categories. Not cross-checked against an in-game screen.

## 5. Other scoring models for the rank thresholds; newer tests since Ver. 1.1.0 and 2.0.0

### Takeaway
ぼってぃ's additive mapping (9+ 最高, 6–8 めちゃイイ, 4–5 ちょっといい, 0–3 ふつう) is still the only rank model built from play, and its author calls it a feel. The alternatives are a tag-match percentage with no source (PokoTraqr) and invented numbers (pokopia.center). Two players independently agree on the Environment-Level points per rank (10/5/2/1, ×2 for legendaries). No newer test was found. Patches 1.1.0, 2.0.0 and 2.0.1 changed the display and the noticing behaviour, not the scoring.

### Cited Findings
- **[Community-tested hypothesis, ぼってぃ, Ver. 1.0.1]** 「完全に体感の域を出ないが…最高：9以上 / めちゃイイ感じ：6～8 / ちょっといい感じ：4～5 / ふつう：0～3 / びみょう：マイナス？」 (purely a feel, but: Awesome 9+, Great 6–8, Nice 4–5, Average 0–3, Iffy negative?). "Within the range tested, totals of 9 or more all seem to be 最高." Data points in the same note: Pichu in an empty grass habitat → ふつう at once (possibly the roof shade counted as Dark). Swirlix newly housed → **びみょう** with nothing disliked present. Swirlix given its favourite food → ふつう. Toy blocks placed between the two homes → both ちょっといい感じ. Machoke given favourite sour berries for 7 days → never above ちょっといい感じ, then 最高 the day environment, toys and decorations were added. Pichu given a plain Leppa Berry → no change, and a sweet favourite food the same day → no change (the day's meal slot seemed used up). — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Community-tested, ぼってぃ]** The tester's own list of untested points: whether a bad path to the Pokémon Center hurts; whether a built house helps; legendary weight; the 「・・・」 bubble; whether a Pokémon never fed can reach 最高; time of day and weather; food from a basket. — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Community-tested, two independent sources]** Environment points per rank: 最高 10, めちゃイイ 5, ちょっといい 2, ふつう 1, びみょう and no home 0 (ぼってぃ, solved from two towns totalling 93). PokoTraqr gives the same, crediting u/mogsetsu on r/Pokopia, with legendaries and mythicals doubled (20/10/4/2) and "Ditto is scored the same as regular Pokemon". ぽこポケ学会 links a Japanese translation of Mogsetsu's research. — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440); [PokoTraqr calculator](https://www.pokotraqr.com/paradise/env-calculator); [ぽこポケ学会](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/)
- **[Unverified]** PokoTraqr's comfort tiers "Iffy (0-24%) … Awesome (90%+)" of tags matched. No test; inconsistent with ぼってぃ's additive data. — [PokoTraqr Housing Guide](https://www.pokotraqr.com/guides/housing-guide)
- **[Guide]** pokopia.center's comfort guide (2026-03-24): "There is no published official threshold table for how many objects move a Pokemon between ratings… Ask and recheck the PC instead of relying on invented formulas." — [pokopia.center](https://pokopia.center/posts/pokopia-comfort-level-furniture-guide-2026/)
- **[Unverified anecdotes, Ver. 1.0.4]** ろぜ: Froakie and Drifblim stuck at めちゃイイ reached 最高 after 2 days. Marill stayed at めちゃイイ for 5 days with no wish, then reached 最高 within minutes of moving to a different house. One Pokémon dropped from 最高 to めちゃイイ the next day after a campfire was placed (Oddish), while Gloom and Vileplume next to it stayed at 最高. Game8 JP reader comment: Pawmi and Pawmo at めちゃイイ never name a wish, and a Leppa Berry changes nothing. — [ろぜ](https://note.com/roze_flare999/n/nc89b8a22469e); [Game8 JP comments](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767513)
- **[Official, via guide]** Ver. 1.1.0 (2026-06-10 JP): 「環境レベルの詳細画面で、その日に「すみごこち」のお願いを叶えたポケモンに「✅」が付くように変更」 (a ✅ now marks Pokémon whose comfort wish was granted that day). Ver. 2.0.0 (2026-08-05): the furniture-removed notice change. **Ver. 2.0.1 (2026-09-08): bug fixes only, such as moving kits that couldn't be picked up.** DLC Part 2 「どきどきデコレーション」 (9 Dec) needs Ver. 3.0.0. — [攻略大百科: アップデート一覧](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/3507); [Serebii: Patches](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/patch.shtml)

### Inferences
- ぼってぃ's own data contains a Swirlix at びみょう with zero points and nothing disliked, so 0–3 doesn't always mean Average for a newcomer. App check: the README's "it's a ceiling; a newcomer starts at Iffy or Average" already covers this. The scoring table itself (0–3 Average) can stay as the ceiling.
- Swirlix's step to ちょっといい感じ (favourite food 2 + one toy) is 3 points under the model, which would predict Average. It fits only if the blocks were a Swirlix favourite or its environment was met. The tester does not say. This is the one data point that sits uneasily with the mapping.
- Because no test postdates Ver. 1.0.4, the model's version status is "untested since 1.1.0, but no patch note touches scoring".

### Gaps
- No datamine, official table or Reddit post with per-item scores was found. The original r/Pokopia Mogsetsu post was unreachable (Reddit is blocked), so its comfort content, if any, is unknown.
- Whether a Pokémon never fed can reach 最高 is untested. The app's maximum without food is 10, which would allow it.

## 6. Does house type matter (kit vs block vs habitat; りっぱな kits; bigger is easier)? Is it a score bonus or only a speed or space effect?

### Takeaway
Every source agrees a house beats a habitat and a bigger or grand kit house is "easier". The reasons given are speed, room for more items, and insulation from outdoor weather and light. Nobody measures a fixed score bonus. One guide says a built house "guarantees a minimum comfort".

### Cited Findings
- **[Guide, Serebii]** "If a Pokémon is in a built house rather than just a habitat, their Comfy Level will increase quicker than if they are just in a habitat." — [Serebii: Environment & Comfy Level](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/environmentlevel.shtml)
- **[Guide, Game8 JP]** 「ポケモンの住み心地は小さい家やブロックで囲んで作った家よりも、なるべく良い家に住まわせたほうが上がりやすい… 「◯◯のりっぱなキット」で作れる高級な家に引っ越しさせたポケモンは住み心地が最高になりやすい」 (comfort rises more easily in a good house than a small or block house; Pokémon in the shop's grand-kit houses reach 最高 easily). 「住んでいる家が大きいほど、すみごこちも上がりやすいです」 (the bigger the house, the easier comfort rises). — [Game8 JP: すみごこち](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767513); [Game8 JP: 引っ越し](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767983)
- **[Guide, 攻略大百科]** りっぱな houses 「家は広く、複数のポケモンが住むことができ、大量の家具も置けるため、非常にすみごこちを上げやすい」 (roomy, several residents and lots of furniture, so comfort is very easy to raise). The reason given is space. Dark-loving Pokémon are easier to satisfy in a house. The environment-level page adds 「家を建ててポケモンのすみかに設定すると、最低限のすみごこちが保証されます」 (making a built house its home guarantees a minimum comfort). — [攻略大百科: すみごこち](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/3509); [攻略大百科: 環境レベル上げ](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/1760)
- **[Community-tested hypothesis, ぽこポケ学会]** 「(仮説)キットの家 > 家 > 生息地」 (hypothesis: kit house > block house > habitat), ranked as the second-weakest factor. — [ぽこポケ学会](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/)
- **[Official tip via ぼってぃ; untested]** The in-game tips list 「建築した家」 (a built house) as a plus factor, but ぼってぃ lists 「建築した家のポジティブな影響の有無」 (whether a built house has a positive effect) as unverified. — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Guide, Game8 EN]** "A Pokemon's comfort level increases faster once they move out of their original habitat and into a house of their own… A house also tends to have more room for you to add decorations." In a habitat "you're limited by the space". — [Game8: How to Increase Comfort Level](https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Pokopia/archives/583142)
- **[Unverified anecdote]** ろぜ's Marill: 5 days stuck at めちゃイイ in a habitat, then 最高 within minutes of moving into a house. がんも reached high comfort with simple block houses and a leaf den. — [ろぜ](https://note.com/roze_flare999/n/nc89b8a22469e); [がんも](https://note.com/ganmo_umai/n/nc20c24282d4d)
- **[Guide, Serebii]** Kits and buildings raise the **Environment Level** "even if a Pokémon doesn't immediately live there". This is a separate effect from comfort. — [Serebii: Environment & Comfy Level](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/environmentlevel.shtml)

### Inferences
- The evidence reads as speed, space and insulation. A house gives room for more tagged and favourite items and shields residents from rain putting out fires, from daylight for dark-lovers, and so on. It may also set a floor, per 攻略大百科's "minimum guaranteed". Moving house also forces a re-check (re-noticing), which may explain the Marill anecdote.
- App check: adding no points for house type fits the evidence. If anything is added, it should be a floor ("at least Average in a built house") or a note, not points toward the 9.

### Gaps
- No controlled test of the same Pokémon with the same items in a habitat, a block house and a kit house.

## 7. What the in-game tips (くらしのヒント), Pokédex and item screens say

### Takeaway
The only report of the in-game tip list is ぼってぃ's paraphrase. It names favourite environment, rest furniture, food, toys, decorations and a built house as plus factors, plus legendaries' larger weight. Item details show the tag. The Pokédex shows favourites, favourite environment and flavour. The daily wish names a category, sometimes with a favourite attached.

### Cited Findings
- **[Official, paraphrased by ぼってぃ]** 「公式情報（くらしのヒント）だと 加点要素：好きな環境 / 休憩判定の家具を置く / 食べ物をあげる / おもちゃを置く / 飾り付けを置く / 建築した家 / 伝説、幻はレベルへの影響大」 (plus factors in the in-game life tips: favourite environment, placing rest furniture, giving food, placing toys, placing decorations, a built house, legendaries and mythicals weigh heavily on the level). Minus factors (「と思われるもの」, what the tester takes them to be): placing disliked things, and an unpaved path to the Pokémon Center. No verbatim screenshot transcription was found elsewhere. — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Official, via guide]** The item details screen (bag or workbench) shows the tag: 「「おもちゃ」「かざりつけ」「きゅうけい」など、その家具の特性を確認できます」 (you can see the item's trait, such as toy, decoration or rest). Serebii's item pages show Category (Furniture, Utilities, Outdoor, Other, Blocks, Nature), Tag (Relaxation, Toy, Decoration or none), flags such as Fire, and Favorite Categories. — [攻略大百科](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/3509); [Serebii: Antique bed](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items/antiquebed.shtml)
- **[Official, via guides]** The Pokédex lists 「好きな環境」 (favourite environment) and 「好きなもの」 (favourites), with the favourite flavour (あまい, からい, …) among the favourites. Nintendo Life says the entry covers "the flavour of food they prefer, the types of games and toys they enjoy, and the weather they like the best". ぼってぃ's comfort check is the only in-game way they found to confirm that a placed item matches. — [Game8 JP](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767513); [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/pokemon-pokopia-how-to-raise-comfort-levels); [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Official, via guides]** 「すみごこち どう？」 gives the current rank and **one wish per day** (reset 05:00), for example 「「かざりつけ」を欲しがっている」 (it wants a decoration). Granting it raises comfort **one rank**. Wishes can name food, an environment change, or a tagged item, and ろぜ's Honchkrow asked for a favourite + tag combination (キラキラの休憩). A hungry Pokémon always asks for food first. The PC (− button) lists everyone's rank, and since Ver. 1.1.0 it marks ✅ for wishes granted that day. — [Game8 JP](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767513); [攻略大百科](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/3509); [ぽこポケ学会](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/); [ろぜ](https://note.com/roze_flare999/n/nc89b8a22469e); [攻略大百科: アップデート一覧](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/3507)
- **[Community-tested, ぼってぃ]** Comfort changes only once the Pokémon notices: it says 「見てみるよ」 (I'll take a look) then 「ありがとう」 (thanks). Environment, food and the comfort check are noticed at once, "half-forced", while placed items may need the Pokémon on screen, leading it there, or a reload. — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Guide]** Other plus inputs in guides, none with point values: completing requests (Game8 EN); playing mini-games with a Pokémon ("After you play these games, their comfort levels will increase", Game8 EN); talking to a 「…」 bubble (攻略大百科). ぼってぃ tested the bubble: a heart appeared but comfort stayed ふつう. Other guide inputs: a full stomach (GameWith: 「お腹いっぱいになったり、好みの食べ物を食べたりすることで」); a paved road to the Pokémon Center (Game8 JP, GameWith; ぼってぃ's blocking test showed no change); environment rocks (しめったいわ for Humid, あついいわ Warm, つめたいいわ Cool, さらさらいわ Dry; narunikki); and, since Ver. 2.0.0, a following Scrub Pokémon cleaning residents (Serebii). — [Game8: Comfort](https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Pokopia/archives/583142); [攻略大百科: 環境レベル上げ](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/1760); [GameWith](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/545675); [Game8 JP: 環境レベル](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767551); [narunikki](https://narunikki.hatenablog.com/entry/2026/04/13/120000); [Serebii](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/environmentlevel.shtml); [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)

### Inferences
- The tip list's named inputs (environment, rest, food, toy, decoration, built house) match the app's factors one for one, except the built house. It names no favourite-only factor beyond "favourite environment", which slightly favours the app's "favourites through the trio" rule.
- Requests, games, the 「…」 bubble and cleaning are event-based nudges that a layout planner cannot see. They belong with "it's a ceiling" rather than in the score.

### Gaps
- No verbatim in-game くらしのヒント text or screenshot transcription was found beyond ぼってぃ's paraphrase.
- No source checks whether the game ever states numbers, caps or a favourite count in any menu.
