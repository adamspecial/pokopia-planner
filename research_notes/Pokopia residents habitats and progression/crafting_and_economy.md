Stamp Rally: 50 (unevolved), 100 (fully evolved), 200 (Legendary), 1000 (Mew) per stamp; redeem on Fridays. Selling is done at the PC, not an NPC. No per-item sell price list found. Battling gives no coins; Trade specialty barters items not coins. — thegameswiki life-coins, dittobase life coin farming guide (search summaries)
- [Guide] PC shop rotating stock resets daily 5:00 AM (thegameswiki); GameFAQs players report limited kits not restocking for a week+, maybe only after full buyout and at certain Environment Level (unverified). — fextralife PC_Shop, gamefaqs 81123474
- [Guide] Prices: Packing Tips Rookie 100 or 300 (conflict), Intermediate 500, Pro 1,000, Expert 1,200 or 1,500 (conflict); PP Up 300 (or 100); Handy Bag 500 (Withered Wasteland Env Lv 3); Pokémon Center rebuilding kit 1,000; seeds and small kits 10-50.
- [Guide] Bag: 20 slots at start; Rookie +10, Intermediate +30, Pro +30, Expert +30 = 120; Handy Bag (サブバッグ) +10 quick slots = 130. Stack 99 per slot. Region unlocks: Rookie WW, Intermediate Rocky Ridges, Pro Bleak Beach, Expert Sparkling Skylands (one wiki; another changelog swaps Intermediate/Pro). JP: 初級 300コイン +10, 中級 500 +30. — game8.jp 767463, gamewith 545920, thegameswiki packing-tips-books
- [Guide] Storage Box 1 Lumber, 20 slots; Big Storage Box 3 Pokémetal, 60 slots (Register 30 Pokémon, JP says obtained in ドンヨリうみべの街); Gulpin 30, Swalot 90 (thegameswiki). Ordinary boxes don't share contents (gamewith 547203 / pokemonza).
- [Official/guide] Ver. 2.0.0 (5 Aug 2026, free) added きょうゆうそうち (shared storage device): same contents wherever placed, including other towns; Cloud Island devices share only among Cloud Island devices; recipe bought for 3,000 coins after raising まっさらな街 env level (Lv 8 per one blog). Capacity 140 (20x7, preview play, ポケモン速報 X) vs 80 (Zaikei citing JP guides) — conflict. — game8.jp 803649, altema kyouyuusouchi, automaton 20260805-458470, zaikei 20260807/864765
- [Community-tested] Workbench (クラフト台) uses contents of storage boxes adjacent orthogonally (not diagonal), max 4 boxes per workbench (あしやひろ X test). Works with an empty bag. — x.com/kk6/status/2033939774445391899, gamewith X 2030455965330362403, kengomon craftbox
- [Community-tested?] Crafting inside a house lets all storage boxes in the house be used (blog ぴぴこ). — ukatu-na-pipico.blog.jp/archives/34214351
- [Guide] Rocks broken become small 'stone' items; Dream Island objects respawn every visit (daily); rocks can be copied with the Pokémon Center 3D printer. Cut trees do not regrow by themselves (hugkurashi; conflicting blog says they do); planted trees full size after 3 days. — game8.jp 768001, 769376, hugkurashi
