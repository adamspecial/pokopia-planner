Scope and method: two research passes. Pass 1 (cut off) used WebSearch summaries only. Pass 2 (2026-11-23) fetched Serebii, GameWith, Game8 (JP and EN), kyu49 (ぽこポケ学会) and pokopia.dev pages in full with curl. WebFetch could not resolve serebii.net, but curl could. ## 1. Crafting stations: what each makes, timing, batch crafting, power/fuel/Pokémon needs

### Takeaway
The workbench makes everything from known recipes. You choose a quantity, and no source describes a timer. It draws materials from the bag plus up to 4 orthogonally adjacent storage boxes (since Ver. 2.0.0, boxes first), a shared-storage device and following Gulpin/Swalot. Processing stations all need a Pokémon specialty nearby. The smelting furnace and metallic furnace take one material type at a time and need a Burn Pokémon. The concrete mixer needs a Crush Pokémon and water. The community box feeds Gather/Sort and processing Pokémon. Cooking tools need a fire source. None of the processing stations needs power. No source mentions fuel for smelting.

### Inferences
- Since every source describes choosing a quantity and getting the items, the planner can model workbench crafting as instant with a quantity selector. Queueing is unconfirmed.
- For material reach, model the workbench as: bag + up to 4 orthogonal boxes + any adjacent shared-storage device + following Storage Pokémon. Boxes and the shared device are consumed before the bag (Ver. 2.0.0+).

### Gaps
- No source states outright whether workbench crafting takes time, or a per-craft cap (e.g. 99).
- No source says how many furnaces one Burn Pokémon can run at once. A furnace's input capacity (how many ore can be loaded) was not found. The 99-item tests below suggest at least 99.

## 2. Smelting and processing: inputs, outputs, ratios, times, fuel

### Takeaway
Smelting is 1 ore -> 1 product: iron, copper and gold ore to ingots; Pokémetal and Rare Pokémetal fragments to Pokémetal and Rare Pokémetal; beach sand, sandstone or volcanic ash to glass. It runs in real time, and the metallic furnace is exactly 2x faster. Measured times conflict: 2 min/1 min versus 4 min/2 min per item. Which Burn Pokémon you use makes no difference. No fuel is consumed. GameWith says concrete is 1 limestone -> 4 concrete, against data.json's 1:1. Advancing the Switch clock finishes any processing.

### Cited Findings
- [Guide] Furnace and metallic furnace inputs -> outputs: Iron ore -> Iron ingot; Copper ore -> Copper ingot; Gold ore -> Gold ingot; Pokémetal fragment -> Pokémetal; Rare Pokémetal fragment -> Rare Pokémetal; beach sand and other sands or sandstone, and volcanic ash -> Glass — [GameWith furnace](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/547415); [GameWith metallic furnace](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/549467); [Game8 EN](https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Pokopia/archives/586265); [Serebii items](https://serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items.shtml) ("Smelt Rare Pokémetal in the furnace"). Glass can also be obtained by smashing broken windows — [Serebii items](https://serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items.shtml)
- [Guide] Conversion is 1:1 (one Iron ore -> one Iron ingot); the bottleneck is time, not ratio; the furnace shows an indicator for the next ingot — Siliconera ingot/glass guide (search summary): [Siliconera](https://www.siliconera.com/how-to-make-ingots-and-glass-in-pokemon-pokopia/)
- [Community-tested, CONFLICT] Time per item: ヘイグ measured 2 min per item in the normal furnace and 1 min in the metallic furnace (h1g.jp "メタルなようこうろ" article, URL not recorded); 納見みどり measured 4 min per item normal (99 items = 6 h 36 min) and 2 min metallic (99 = 3 h 18 min) — [X @nominz](https://x.com/nominz/status/2033745188921938309); see also [X @hirokiykt](https://x.com/hirokiykt/status/2032433259280568346). GameWith: metallic is 2x faster (its "about 40 s" note is the moment of a side-by-side comparison) — [GameWith metallic furnace](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/549467). All agree on the 2x ratio
- [Community-tested] Burn Pokémon don't differ in smelting speed: five Pokémon from different towns and comfort levels timed on the same job showed no difference; only the furnace type matters — [hyperts.net](https://hyperts.net/pokopoke/moyasu/). No difference between Magby and Magmar — [game-tier.com](https://game-tier.com/pokopoke/pocoa-evolution/)
- [Guide] "Multiple fire Pokémon can speed production" (Siliconera/rankedboost search summary) — contradicted by the hyperts test above, and unverified
- [Guide] Advancing the Switch 2 clock skips smelting time; small changes within the same day don't send you home, but crossing a date change returns you to your home — [appmedia](https://appmedia.jp/?p=79804829) (search summary); [GameWith concrete](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/547572) ("時間操作すれば即座に加工が終わる"). One pass-1 source said there is no shortcut for concrete (minority reading, source not recorded)
- [Guide, CONFLICT with data.json] Concrete: 1 Limestone -> 4 Concrete — [GameWith concrete](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/547572) (updated 23 Nov 2026); repeated in [GameWith trade tips](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/545349) ("石灰石1でコンクリ4つ"). Time: "about 1 hour" (GameWith and [gameranbu](https://gameranbu.jp/pocoapokemon/c1fdf37dec61c0c26853), pass 1 summaries) versus one X post giving "1 limestone = 4 pieces, 4 min" (pass 1, URL not recorded). The current GameWith page gives no time. Load as much limestone as possible for long unattended runs; one Crush Pokémon per mixer, so build several mixers to go faster — [GameWith concrete](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/547572)
- [Guide] Concrete demand: story about 95-100; Skylands Pokémon Center 25; Team Initiation Challenge part 4 needs 40 Brick + 20 Gold ingot + 50 Concrete (reward: Rainbow badge) — [GameWith concrete](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/547572); [altema concrete](https://altema.jp/pocoapokemon/concrete) (pass 1)
- [Guide] Recycle: 1 Nonburnable garbage -> 2 Iron ore — GameWith 547416 (pass 1 summary). Glimmet/Glimmora litter iron over time (pass 1)
- [Guide] Tinkagear is made by Tinkmaster (デカヌおやかた) in Sparkling Skylands from Iron ingot — [GameWith metallic furnace](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/549467)
- [Guide] Brick: give Squishy clay to a Burn Pokémon; Paper: Recycle Wastepaper; Concrete: limestone in the mixer with Crush — [Serebii items](https://serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items.shtml) (ratios covered by earlier research)

### Inferences
- The two time measurements differ by exactly 2x, but both show metallic = 1/2 normal. The 4-min reading comes with a consistent 99-item total (99 x 4 min = 396 min = 6 h 36 min), so it is the more fully documented one. A planner could use 4 min normal and 2 min metallic per item, with 2/1 min as the alternative.
- No source mentions fuel for the smelting furnace or mixer; only a Pokémon is needed. The "Furnace kit" in data.json is a power generator that burns things, a different object.

### Gaps
- Concrete mixer time per limestone is unresolved: about 1 h, 4 min, or unstated.
- Times for glass, gold or Pokémetal versus iron: no source says whether they differ by material.
- Batch size per furnace load was not stated anywhere, and no datamined timer table was found.

## 3. Gathering: yields, drop rates and respawn

### Takeaway
Berry trees grow 1 berry per real hour up to 3. Trees give about 4 small logs and don't regrow once cut (one blog disagrees). Glowing blocks and ripples move every day at 05:00. Dream Islands reset fully every day and are the renewable source of ore, limestone, Pokémetal and Rare Pokémetal. GameWith says the Pokémetal ore in each town runs out. In-town ore regeneration is unverified. The 3D printer can turn Pokémetal into more rocks and berry trees.

### Cited Findings
- [Community-tested, multi-source] Berries: 1 per hour per tree, max 3 on a tree; a Headbutt critical drops 4; Headbutt hits up to 10 trees at once; time keeps counting while you are in another area; withering resets the hourly count — [GameWith berries](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/548099); [note harezora_lueur](https://note.com/harezora_lueur/n/nbe66214baa28); [chiebukuro q13326757333](https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q13326757333); one outlier site says "a day". kyu49 adds: berries grow even underwater; no growth while withered (the count probably resets); berries drop to one of 6 fixed spots set by the planting direction; fallen berries are NOT collected by Gather/Sort Pokémon and vanish when you change area — [kyu49 tree](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/tree/)
- [Guide] One felled grown tree gives 4 small logs plus leaves and sturdy sticks; withered trees give fewer logs, so water them first; powered-up Cut (eat bread) plus holding ZR cuts many trees at once — game8.jp 768349 / gamewith 547225 / altema tiisanamaruta (pass 1 search summary; which site said "4" is unclear)
- [Guide, CONFLICT] Planted trees reach full size 3 days after planting; cut trees do not regrow by themselves (hugkurashi blog post 2026/03/11/143851, URL not recorded); a conflicting blog says they do (pass 1)
- [Guide] Broken rocks become small "Stone" items; Dream Island objects respawn on every daily visit; rocks can be copied with the 3D printer — [game8.jp 768001](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/768001); [game8.jp 769376](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/769376)
- [Guide] Dream Islands: one visit per day (show Drifloon a doll); objects and deposits respawn in the same places each day, resetting at 05:00 when the in-game date changes; revisiting the same day doesn't refresh; items left behind are lost — [Serebii Dream Islands](https://serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/dreamislands.shtml); [GameWith daily](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/545468); pokemonza blog 2026/03/07/224118; Shacknews article 148212 ([site](https://www.shacknews.com/), article URL not recorded) (pass 1)

Raikou, Entei, Suicune and Mewtwo can appear — [Serebii Dream Islands](https://serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/dreamislands.shtml). Volcano Dream Island (Arcanine doll) is the most time-efficient iron source — [GameWith 547416](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/547416) (pass 1). ### Inferences
- For the planner, "renewable per day" sources are Dream Islands (one per day), glowing blocks, ripples, the vending machine and Vespiquen. "Renewable per hour" is berries. "Passive" is litter. Town ore should be treated as finite until a test shows otherwise.

### Gaps
- No measured respawn timer exists for town rocks, iron or copper deposits, shells or sea glass. Whether shells and sea glass on the Bleak Beach shoreline come back daily was not found.
- Exact drop rates (e.g. gold versus iron per Arcanine-island deposit) were not found.

## 4. Money, shops, selling and trade

### Takeaway
The currency is Life Coins (ライフコイン/コイン). JP guides agree it comes only from PC challenges and the weekly stamp card. Nothing can be sold for coins. Coins buy recipes, kits, bag upgrades and some materials in the PC shop. Its "today only" stock refreshes at 05:00 and the lineup grows with each town's Environment Level. Trade is a separate barter system with point values at a powered cash register.

### Cited Findings
- [Guide] Life Coins (EN) / コイン (JP). The main source is PC challenges at the Pokémon Center; you must return to the PC to claim; "roughly 50 to 200 per challenge" (one wiki); each completion gives "several hundred" (game8.jp) — thegameswiki life-coins (pass 1 summary); [game8.jp 767358](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767358)
- [Guide] Coins can only be earned by clearing challenges ("チャレンジのクリア以外ではコインを入手できない"), plus the stamp card. Challenges unlock with Env Lv. The Y button reports all completed challenges at once — [GameWith money](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/547206); [game8.jp 767358](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767358) ("金策の方法は、現状はチャレンジのみ")
- [Guide, CONFLICT] Selling: EN wikis (thegameswiki life-coins, pokopia.center) say surplus can be sold at the PC shop; JP guides above say there is no such option; Nintendo Life says Trade uses item "value", not Life Coins (pass 1). Battling gives no coins; the Trade specialty barters items, not coins (dittobase summary, pass 1)
- [Guide] Daily challenges (日がわりチャレンジ) refresh at 05:00 and pay more than normal ones. A weekly Challenge Day (random weekday, often Friday) raises daily-challenge coins — [GameWith daily](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/545468); [game8.jp 767358](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767358); famitsu 68067 (pass 1)
- [Guide, CONFLICT] Stamp card: one stamp per day at the PC; the card runs a week and holds max 5; redeem on Fridays. Values: Basic Pokémon 50, Evolved 100, Legendary 200, Mew 1,000 coins, plus a 1,500-coin bonus for filling all 5. A full card lets you swap a stamp for the day's. Friends' PCs give stamps, capped at 10 unique visitors per island per day — [Serebii stamp card](https://serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/stampcard.shtml). GameWith instead gives examples of Mew stamp 1,300 and round stamp 350, with about 2,000 for 5 stamps; Cloud Island gives +1 — [GameWith 547795](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/547795); [game8.jp 768352](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/768352) (pass 1). ## 5. Cooking and food

### Takeaway

There are 34 dishes: 24 base plus 10 Expansion Pass dishes using sea grapes or watermelon. A dish finishes over time after "りょうり開始". ## 7. Building kits: buying or crafting, build time, Build Pokémon, moving and picking up

### Takeaway
Kits are bought in the PC shop as each area's Env Lv rises (some come from the story). You place one, deliver the materials, and bring the required number of Pokémon including Build. Dens take about 15 min, huts 1 h, cottages and houses finish at the next 05:00. Tinkmaster turns next-day builds into 1 h. Moving and demolishing need kits and a Bulldoze Pokémon, take 15 min fixed each and return the materials. Each area has 40 building points.

## 9. Contradictions and gaps relative to data.json

### Takeaway
Most data.json station recipes and unlocks match the sources. The main conflicts: concrete yield (1:1 in data.json versus 1:4), Rare Pokémetal's origin (missing fragment smelting and shop source), the storage-box unlock count, the Wasteland PC lumber, and an ambiguity between the PC rebuild kit and the 1,000-coin look-change kits.

### Cited Findings
- Concrete: data.json process `"Concrete": ["Limestone", 1]` (1 -> 1) versus 1 Limestone -> 4 Concrete — [GameWith concrete](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/547572); [GameWith trade tips](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/545349)
- Rare Pokémetal: data.json lists it as a raw material, "Rare find while exploring", with no process entry. Sources: smelted from Rare Pokémetal fragments in either furnace — [GameWith furnace](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/547415); sold in every town's shop at Env Lv 7 — [Serebii items](https://serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items.shtml); Rarify, 1 Star Piece -> 1 — [Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rarify_(specialty)). data.json has no "Rare Pokémetal fragment" material
- Pokémetal: data.json lacks the shop source (10-pack for 50 coins at Env Lv 6 in each town) — [GameWith Pokémetal](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/547936); [Serebii items](https://serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/items.shtml)
- Storage Box: data.json "Register 6 Pokémon" (Serebii agrees) versus 7 — [GameWith storage box](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/547203)
- Wasteland Pokémon Center: data.json and Serebii say 10 Lumber; GameWith says 20 Lumber — [GameWith 545330](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/545330)
- Pokémon Center kits: data.json names them "Wasteland/Beach/... Pokémon Center kit" with rebuild materials and "Also costs 1,000 Life Coins". Serebii's kits of those names are the look-change kits ("Use this to remodel a Pokémon Center to make it look just like ...") — [Serebii building](https://serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/building.shtml). GameWith prices those at 1,000 coins at Env Lv 10, and the separate rebuild kit (ポケセンたてなおしキット) at 1,000 coins at Env Lv 3 — [GameWith 545330](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/545330)
- Limestone source text misses the bulk source, the Clefairy-doll Dream Island — [GameWith concrete](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/547572); [Serebii Dream Islands](https://serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/dreamislands.shtml)
- Stardust "99 -> Star Piece" matches Bulbapedia; Game8 EN says 100 (pass 1)
- Paints: data.json says every paint (including Black and White) comes from "Crush berries". Black and White come only as random bonuses — [Serebii paint](https://serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/paint.shtml)
- Consistent with sources: Smelting furnace (4 Iron ore + 4 Stone; unlocks on first iron ingot), Metallic furnace (10 Iron ingot, 3 Pokémetal, 3 Tinkagear; SS Lv 4), Concrete mixer (Conkeldurr), Cash register (Bleak Beach shop), Big Storage Box (30 registrations), Glass inputs (beach sand / sandstone / volcanic ash), the 18 paint-balloon recipes, kit build times (den 15 min, hut 1 h, cottage next day)

### Inferences
- Fixing concrete to 0.25 limestone per concrete would cut planned limestone needs by 4x. Since GameWith repeats 1:4 on two pages updated in Nov 2026, it is the stronger reading.

### Gaps
- data.json has no storage capacities, bag tiers or coin prices; the sections above supply candidates.
