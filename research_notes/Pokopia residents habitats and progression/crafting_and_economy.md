- [Guide] Currency is Life Coins (EN) / コイン (JP). Main source: PC challenges at the Pokémon Center; you must return to the PC to claim; "roughly 50 to 200 per challenge" (one wiki). Stamp Rally: 50 (unevolved), 100 (fully evolved), 200 (Legendary), 1000 (Mew) per stamp; redeem on Fridays. Selling is done at the PC, not an NPC. No per-item sell price list found. Battling gives no coins; Trade specialty barters items not coins. — thegameswiki life-coins, dittobase life coin farming guide (search summaries)
- [Guide] PC shop rotating stock resets daily 5:00 AM (thegameswiki); GameFAQs players report limited kits not restocking for a week+, maybe only after full buyout and at certain Environment Level (unverified). — fextralife PC_Shop, gamefaqs 81123474
- [Guide] Prices: Packing Tips Rookie 100 or 300 (conflict), Intermediate 500, Pro 1,000, Expert 1,200 or 1,500 (conflict); PP Up 300 (or 100); Handy Bag 500 (Withered Wasteland Env Lv 3); Pokémon Center rebuilding kit 1,000; seeds and small kits 10-50.
- [Guide] Bag: 20 slots at start; Rookie +10, Intermediate +30, Pro +30, Expert +30 = 120; Handy Bag (サブバッグ) +10 quick slots = 130. Stack 99 per slot. Region unlocks: Rookie WW, Intermediate Rocky Ridges, Pro Bleak Beach, Expert Sparkling Skylands (one wiki; another changelog swaps Intermediate/Pro). JP: 初級 300コイン +10, 中級 500 +30. — game8.jp 767463, gamewith 545920, thegameswiki packing-tips-books
- [Guide] Storage Box 1 Lumber, 20 slots; Big Storage Box 3 Pokémetal, 60 slots (Register 30 Pokémon, JP says obtained in ドンヨリうみべの街); Gulpin 30, Swalot 90 (thegameswiki). Ordinary boxes don't share contents (gamewith 547203 / pokemonza).

Capacity 140 (20x7, preview play, ポケモン速報 X) vs 80 (Zaikei citing JP guides) — conflict. — game8.jp 803649, altema kyouyuusouchi, automaton 20260805-458470, zaikei 20260807/864765

- [Community-tested?] Crafting inside a house lets all storage boxes in the house be used (blog ぴぴこ). — ukatu-na-pipico.blog.jp/archives/34214351
- [Guide] Rocks broken become small 'stone' items; Dream Island objects respawn every visit (daily); rocks can be copied with the Pokémon Center 3D printer. One SEO site mentions a "Golden Crafting Bench" with no timer: not corroborated anywhere, treat as false.
- [Guide] Cooking: Cutting board -> salads (Leaf + 1 other); Cooking pot on a stove -> soups (needs >=1 Fresh Water); Frying pan on a stove -> hamburger steaks (needs >=1 Bean); Bread oven -> breads (needs Wheat; Torkoal teaches it; Burn Pokémon needed to light it the first time). Category -> move boost: salad Leafage, soup Water Gun, bread Cut, hamburger Rock Smash. Recipe count 24 (launch-era guides) vs 34 (game8.jp 767692, pokebros "全34種"); likely more added later (unconfirmed). Conflict: Simple Bread Wheat x3 vs Wheat x1 + 2 any. — bulbapedia Cooking_(Pokopia), nintendolife cooking guide, game8.jp 767692
- [Guide] Move buff from food ends when its PP is used up, not by time (one JP guide); another says duration under investigation. — game8.jp 767692 / gamewith 545733 (search summary)
- [Guide] Flavours: Bulbapedia lists 6 (spicy, dry, sweet, bitter, sour, mild/クセなし); some guides list 5. Each Pokémon has 好きな味; feeding/placing matching food raises comfort (すみごこち) and friendship. Ingredient flavours: Chesto Dry, Pecha Sweet, Rawst Bitter, Aspear Sour, Carrot Spicy, Chili sauce Spicy, Seaweed Bitter, Potato Bitter; Bean Sweet (JP) ; Wheat Dry (JP); Tomato Sour; Leppa (ナナシ) Sour; Oran? ヒメリ (Leppa is ヒメリ; ナナシ is Aspear) -> check. — bulbapedia Flavor_(Pokopia), altema osonaemono
- [Guide, consistent across >=4 sites] Mosslax (こけカビゴン) offering, once per day, lasts until 5:00 next morning; effect by flavour: Sweet = ancient artifacts/relics easier to find; Spicy = Pokémon appear in habitats more; Bitter = rare items easier to find; Dry = Ho-Oh / Lugia seen more, rainbow & silver feathers easier; Sour = better shop stock (one source adds discount, unverified); Mild = friendship rises faster. Cooked dishes give a stronger effect than raw ingredients. — altema osonaemono, gamepedia 2473, game.mirai-media.net pokoa-kokekabigon, x.com/UniteVids/status/2032775019760124270

Volcano Dream Island (かざんのゆめしま) is the most time-efficient iron source; needs ウインディにんぎょう (Arcanine doll). — pokemonza 2026/03/07/224118, shacknews 148212, gamewith 547416, zatudanjouzu iron

Rocky Ridges has iron blocks in the town walls. No timer measured. — thegameswiki mining, pokopiahabitats rare materials (search summaries)
- [Guide] Recycle: 1 nonburnable garbage -> 2 iron (gamewith 547416). (data.json says Recycle -> Iron ore without a ratio; earlier notes cover Recycle ratios.) Litter Klawf? no: キラーメ / キラフロル (Glimmet / Glimmora) litter iron over time.
- [Guide] Gold ore and glowing stone need powered-up Rock Smash (from eating hamburger steak; beans needed). — thegameswiki mining, wikipokopia ore-farming
- [Community-tested, multi-source] Berries: 1 berry per hour per tree, max 3 on a tree; Headbutt critical drops 4; Headbutt hits up to 10 trees at once (GameWith); time counts while you are in another area; if the tree withers even briefly the hour count resets. One site says "a day" (outlier). — gamewith 548099, note.com/harezora_lueur/n/nbe66214baa28, chiebukuro q13326757333, kyu49 tree page
- [Guide] Planted trees reach full size 3 days after planting; cut trees do not regrow on their own (conflicting blog). — hugkurashi 2026/03/11/143851
- [Guide] Pokémon Center 3D printer: photograph an object (camera object mode), register photo, pay Pokémetal or Rare Pokémetal per copy; choose quantity; no daily limit; 20 photo slots. Examples: sprinkler 4 Pokémetal; non-native berry tree (e.g. Rawst) 1 Rare Pokémetal; stylish door 10 Pokémetal; air conditioner 1 Rare Pokémetal. Items with a 'forbidden' mark can't be copied. Rocks and berry trees can be copied. — gamewith 548469, gamepedia 3085, note.com/game9820/n/n28d8dc3b9cb7
- [Guide] Build times: den about 15 min; data.json: hut 1 h, cottage next day. GameWith: with Tinkmaster (デカヌおやかた, Engineer) as lead, 'next day (5:00)' builds finish in 1 hour; '2 hours' -> 1 hour; needs story progress to Sparkling Skylands. More Build helpers / higher friendship said to speed builds (unverified). Advancing the Switch clock finishes builds. — gamewith 545330, 545874, deltiasgaming, pokodexapp accelerate-construction-time
- [Guide] Moving/demolishing (解体/引っ越し) needs one Bulldoze (じならし) Pokémon; demolition 15 min fixed and rebuild 15 min (about 30 min real time total); house must have no Pokémon inside and nothing attached outside; materials and the original kit are returned almost entirely. Before materials are put in, a kit can be picked up with Y and re-placed. Remodel (改築): place a new kit so the old building fits in its range, signboard touching. — gamewith 547850, games.nen5tare.com pocoapoke-kenchiku-yarikata, pokemonza 2026/03/12/012322
- [Official] Paint: official site page "色やガラを変える" (22 May 2026). Smeargle (ドーブル, Bleak Beach) recolours: hold the furniture, talk, choose 'ペイントして!'; for a house, lead Smeargle to the house. Needs paint (えのぐ) from giving berries to a Crush Pokémon ('えのぐにして'). Only items with a brush icon can be painted; number of paint marks = number of separately paintable parts. Unlocked by the request 「色をぬってもらおう！」. Patterns (柄) learned via Smeargle's requests; gamepedia 5950 notes new patterns added and painting underwater possible (update). Paint balloons colour furniture but can't do per-part or patterns. — pocoapokemon.jp/ja/howtoplay/260522_02/, gamewith 549794, game8.jp 769142, gamepedia 5950
- [Guide] Paint from berries: e.g. 1 Leppa? (ヒメリのみ) x1 -> red paint x5 (gamepedia 2988 sample). Six colours made reliably by Crush: momon (Pecha) -> pink etc. White and black paint come only as random bonus when giving berries. Orange has no berry: mix red+yellow (paint balloon recipe) or give Smeargle several paints to mix. — gamewith 545836, gamepedia 2988, pokemongo-raku post154435
- [Guide] Trade (とりひき): powered cash register on a (wide) table -> a random Trade Pokémon opens a barter shop; goods appear over time (advance the clock to restock); each good has a point price; you offer up to 5 kinds of items whose total points cover it; items the trader likes count 1.5x; one Trade Pokémon can run at most 2 registers (2 goods) at once; trades may not start immediately after setup. Register recipe: Bleak Beach shop after Env Lv 2 (data.json: Iron ore 1). Chansey (ピンプク? Happiny) at PC register. — game8.jp 768500, gamewith 547898, 547156, ndw.jp pokopoke-game-260319, note.com/game9820/n/n310c91660a94
