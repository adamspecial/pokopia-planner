## Interim raw findings (to be merged)
- Serebii specialty list verbatim descriptions fetched (serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/specialty.shtml). "Each Pokémon can have up to two of these specialties."
- Serebii building.shtml: Tinkaton (Tinkmaster) leading a kit build: "a Next Day build becomes just 1 hour". 40 building points per area; each build 1 or 2 points.
- kyu49 limitation.md: kit houses 40 per area (big houses with internal map switch count 2); max 14 builds in progress at once (incl. windmills). Lift 13-65 tiles, slope max 50%, station 5x4x5, cross-section 3x5 flat / 3x6 sloped.
- kyu49 clock.md: after building, Pokémon walk back near home; Pokémon hitting magma move to nearby footing, else respawn at Pokémon Center, then walk shortest path to closest point to completed house.
- kyu49 others.md: Ho-Oh/Lugia open doors; road made of wall-art blocks not recognised as road.
- gamepedia 1611 (updated 2026-09-15): kit build waits 15 min to next 05:00 day change; Tinkmaster 1 day -> 1 hour.
- Game8 EN 582704 (updated 2026-08-06): Water, Grow, Gather Pokémon may get hungry mid-job, stop, and ask for food; any food restarts.
- Game8 JP 769153: night 19:00-4:59; Pokémon may be asleep at night; wake on talk.
- note splatoonweapon (2026-04-02, 04-10, 04-11): ~21 displayed (20-25); AI = stay near home, visit friends/Ditto, hot spring, go to shop with register; action range limit; Litter works while not in town; honey 1 per drop, stock 1; drops over display cap stocked internally; Gather acts on items it sees, random low frequency; Gather must be in town (displayed).
- Game8 EN 585501 (2026-04-06): 22-24 loaded per biome; honey summons but may despawn others; homeless can't be honeyed; sleep on Relaxation furniture at certain times; roads let Pokémon travel further.
- Serebii trade.shtml: Trade Pokémon set up shop at powered register + table (incl. Poké Mart, Pokémon Center when no event); items scale with countertop size; up to 4 different item types paid; favourite-matching items +50% points; you can end trading but cannot force start.
- gamepedia 4307 (2026-06-11): Pokémon Center 6+ items, Poké Mart max 6, register+desk 1+ (desk size); Generate-powered register dies after a day so use poles; trade may not start after game start / time change: change area or wait. Ver 1.1.0 event cancel option.
- Serebii pokemoncenter: Trade Pokémon "occasionally" set up shop in Center, items vary by day; PP heal once per Center per day; PC rebuild needs 8 Pokémon incl. Build, Bulldoze, Chop.
- Serebii cooking: helper specialties change dish (Chop, Crush, Generate, Recycle, Water, Burn, Explode); Chef Dente partner frequently doubles meal.
- Serebii dreamislands: Drifloon, once per day, doll chooses island; island resets end of day.
- Serebii CDs: CD player audible within short range; DJ Rotom holds up to 5 CDs, plays on loop whenever you're around; following DJ Rotom = constant music.
- Serebii dive: 95 underwater-capable Pokémon: 86 full swimming, 9 walking on floor only (Shellos both forms, Gastrodon both forms, Smearguru/Smeargle, Tinkmaster/Tinkaton, Pincurchin, Wiglett, Wugtrio). Underwater crops need Water specialty bubbles or Bubble Machine.
- Game8 JP 768614 (2026-11-23): max 5 followers; hungry Pokémon may refuse; separated ones stay put; large can't pass narrow gaps; flying ignore gaps; walking speeds differ; Up on D-pad to recruit; hold Up to dismiss all.
- Game8 EN 585561: Grow led to plants: flowers bloom instantly; crops and trees +1 stage.
- Game8 JP 768602: mood raised by DJ Rotom CDs, Hype near playing Rotom, relics in Rocky Ridges, some furniture (balloons, stage, party wall deco), party plates with food; 100 points needed for Chef Dente's request.
