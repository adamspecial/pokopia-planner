## Cloud Island (raw notes so far)
- Up to 4 players at once online — Nintendo support 71349 (search summary) https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/71349/kw/pokopia
- With no one on the island, the next arrival becomes host; the island can be played by anyone with its address and Link Code (if set) while the owner is away — Nintendo support 71349 (search summary)
- One guide claims a slot is always kept for the host (so 3 if the host is away); Nintendo's wording contradicts this — search summary, guide unnamed
- No limit on total participants over time (devs, via a guide) — search summary
- GameShare: only 2 players at a time — search summary (Nintendo support 71349 likely)
- Cloud Island has its own Environment Level, shop and challenges. Crafted or bought items stay on it; Trainer Rank doesn't rise there — search summary (Game Rant / Serebii)
- Recipes carry back to the main save; Pokédex entries stay but first finds there don't count as fully discovered in the main dex — search summary
- Main-game Pokémon can't go to a Cloud Island and vice versa — search summary
- Serebii: items from the main story don't carry over; Pokédex and recipe lists stay — https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/cloudislands.shtml (search summary)
- GameFAQs: permanent upgrades (moves, bag expansions, PP size) carry over with recipes, stamps and habitat info — https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/559184-pokemon-pokopia/81129111 (search summary, unverified)
- 2.0.0 fix: players other than the administrator could place a relocation kit on Cloud Islands — search summary of the 2.0.0 notes (Insider Gaming / Pokopia wiki)

## Patch history (raw notes so far)
- 1.0.3: 8 or 9 Apr 2026; 9 bug fixes incl. Dream Island travel stranding players in Palette Town, Cloud Island creation, Pokédex search for Pokémon whose habitat vanished, dark screen between towns, vibration — search summary (Shacknews / Nintendo Life)
- 1.0.4: 22 or 23 Apr 2026; Pokémon Centers can be moved during in-game events; PC freeze with certain saves fixed; Bleak Beach request and companion fixes — search summary (Game8 596281)
- 1.1.0: 9 Jun 2026 (Nintendo Life) / 10 Jun (PLDH X post). Electric items cap 512 to 1,024 (only items placed after the update); laser sensor shows range; volcanic rock blocks on Volcanic Dream Islands; trades can be cancelled or paused at the Pokémon Center during in-game events; Eeveelutions sleep at sofa edges; fixes to Cloud Island Vespiquen honey exchange, host-transfer item loss, guest purchases, repeated event messages, jump-rope contest on Cloud Island — search summary (Game8 605000, Nintendo Life, PLDH)
- 2.0.0: 4 Aug 2026 (5 Aug in some sources). Dive (needs Surf + Bleak Beach request "Raise the environment level!"); building underwater; Strength underwater; partners follow during Surf/Glide/Rollout/Magnet Rise; portal pod (shared storage across areas); safe; Ditto print; 5 hair colours; emotes Lie Down and Ready, Set, Go!; generators 64 to 128 (furnaces excluded); town photo display slots 10 to 40; bulk move between box and bag; crafting takes from the box next to the workbench first; waste-container items can be deleted; habitat residents return if the same habitat is rebuilt nearby; lost relics appraised up to 10 at once; Paint/Gather Honey/Collect repeatable in one interaction; duplicate cooking-pot exploit fixed; Cloud Island relocation-kit fix — search summary (Game8 614179, Shacknews, Forbes, Insider Gaming, pokopiawiki)
- 2.0.1: known issues listed: Magnet Rise may make Pokémon scold the player; loading may hang returning from a Dream Island — https://www.pokeos.com/p/pokopia/patch-notes (search summary)
