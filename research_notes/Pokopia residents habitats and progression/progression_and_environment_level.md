Researched 2026-11-23. Evidence labels: [official] Nintendo/in-game text or official patch notes; [datamined]; [community-tested] someone measured it in game (test linked); [guide] stated by a guide without a test; [unverified] one post, anecdote or a page seen only through search snippets. Current game version at research time: 2.0.0 (5 August 2026) per Serebii's patch page; Expansion Pass Part 1 (Bubbly Basin) is live. Japanese town names: パサパサこうやの街 = Withered Wastelands, ドンヨリうみべの街 = Bleak Beach, ゴツゴツやまの街 = Rocky Ridges, キラキラうきしまの街 = Sparkling Skylands, まっさらな街 = Palette Town, ブクブクうみぞこの街 = Bubbly Basin, クラウド島 = Cloud Island. ## Environment level: point sources, thresholds, cap, decay

### Takeaway
Environment level (1 to 10, "Max" at 10, separate per town) is the sum of the comfort points of the Pokémon living in that town. Two independent community tests agree on 10 / 5 / 2 / 1 points for Awesome / Great / Nice / Average (0 for Iffy and homeless), doubled for Legendary/Mythical residents, with cumulative thresholds 3, 12, 26, 52, 77, 100, 120, 140, 160 for Lv. 2-10; the level can fall, but only after the total drops some margin below the threshold.

### Cited Findings
- [guide] Environment level (環境レベル) of a town is set by the total of the comfort (すみごこち) of the Pokémon living in that town: "環境レベルは街に住んでいるポケモン達の「すみごこち」の合計で決まります" — [Game8 JP, 環境レベルの効率的な上げ方](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767551)
- [guide] Listed ways to raise it: build habitats and befriend Pokémon; solve requests (おねがいごと); ask "すみごこち どう？" and do what the Pokémon wants (furniture, darkening the area, etc.); give a dish of the Pokémon's favourite flavour via "これ見て！"; level the path to the Pokémon Center (fill holes, add stairs) so Pokémon can go out; befriend Mythical/Legendary Pokémon, which "have a larger effect on environment level than other Pokémon"; move Pokémon into high-grade houses (shop "◯◯のりっぱなキット" kits) where comfort tends to max out — [Game8 JP](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767551)
- [guide] Each area has its own Environment Level, from 1 up to 10 — [Serebii, Environment Level & Comfy Level](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/environmentlevel.shtml)
- [guide] Serebii: raise it by making as many Pokémon in the area happy (Comfy Level); completing Requests also raises it "such as Restoring the Pokémon Center"; "If you add buildings and other kits, it will also help raise it even if a Pokémon doesn't immediately live there"; a Legendary or Mythical Pokémon with raised Comfy Level makes it rise quicker — [Serebii](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/environmentlevel.shtml)
- [guide] Comfy Level: a Pokémon with no habitat or home counts as 0; five tiers Awesome, Great, Nice, Average, Iffy; a Pokémon in a built house rises faster than one only in a habitat; disliked things near the habitat lower it; with Bubbly Basin (Expansion Pass Part 1) a Pokémon with the Scrub specialty following you cleans encountered Pokémon, raising their comfy level and friendship — [Serebii](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/environmentlevel.shtml)
- [guide] It can fall. Game8 JP lists three causes: leaving a comfort-raising item or object broken; taking a Pokémon to another map (the town total changes); rain putting out outdoor fires (bonfires) — roof them over — [Game8 JP](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767551)
- [guide] When it falls, the shop lineup added by that level reverts until the level is regained (Game8 JP); Serebii: "all unpurchased items that unlock at that level in the shop are locked again and any challenges from that level are locked once more" — [Game8 JP](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767551); [Serebii](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/environmentlevel.shtml)
- [community-tested] ぼってぃ's in-game test (note.com, 16 March 2026, i.e. version 1.0.x): environment level is the sum of each resident's comfort points (すみごこちpt, the tester's own unofficial name). Points per comfort rank: 最高 (Awesome) 10, めちゃイイ感じ (Great) 5, ちょっといい感じ (Nice) 2, ふつう (Average) 1; びみょう (Iffy) and すみかなし (no home) treated as 0. Derived by solving two towns that showed the same meter: Rocky Ridges 4 Awesome + 5 Great + 13 Nice + 2 Average and Sparkling Skylands 2 Awesome + 8 Great + 15 Nice + 3 Average both total 93 — [ぼってぃ, ぽこあポケモン 環境レベルを検証する](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- [community-tested] Cumulative comfort points needed (same test, Rocky Ridges reset to Lv. 1 then rebuilt): Lv. 2 = 3 (estimated from the meter being about 1/3 full after one Average resident), Lv. 3 = 12, Lv. 4 = 26, Lv. 5 = 52, Lv. 6 = 77, Lv. 7 = 100, Lv. 8 = 120, Lv. 9 = 140, Lv. 10 = 160. So 16 Awesome residents are the minimum for Lv. 10 — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- [community-tested] Hysteresis: when the total falls below a level's threshold the level is kept for a margin, e.g. Lv. 5 (52 pt) was still held at about 50 pt; a meter at 0 is therefore not necessarily the boundary — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440). A calculator crediting Reddit user u/mogsetsu says the level drops only below about 75% progress of that level, and that the progress bar is a linear interpolation between the current and next threshold (unverified; read only via search snippets, site blocked from this session) — [PokoTraqr Environment Level Calculator](https://www.pokotraqr.com/paradise/env-calculator)
- [community-tested, second source] The u/mogsetsu scoring (r/Pokopia, via PokoTraqr and translated on X by あみにし and linked from ぽこポケ学会) gives regular Pokémon and Ditto 10 / 5 / 2 / 1 for Awesome / Great / Nice / Average, the same as ぼってぃ, and Legendary/Mythical Pokémon double: 20 / 10 / 4 / 2; its example path to Lv. 10 totals 160 points — [PokoTraqr](https://www.pokotraqr.com/paradise/env-calculator); [ぽこポケ学会 家と住み心地 (link list)](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/); [あみにし on X](https://x.com/aminisi/status/2034427479033827747)
- [unverified, conflicting] Pokopia Island's calculator describes a different model: 0-8 points per Pokémon, total divided by 8 for the base level, plus a habitat-variety bonus and +5 per Legendary. No test is cited; it disagrees with both tested sources — [Pokopia Island Environment Level Calculator](https://pokopiaisland.com/tools/environment-calculator) (seen only in search snippets)
- [community-tested] Ditto's own house (メタモンの家) has a comfort rating and counts toward environment level; it maxes with 13 kinds of furniture, and only 5 kinds each of toy / rest / decoration are evaluated — [ぽこポケ学会, 家と住み心地](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/)
- [community-tested] Comfort changes only register when the Pokémon notices the change (on-screen, or after you lead it there, or after a town reload); breaking a house did not drop the level at once — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- [community-tested, hypothesis] ぼってぃ's comfort model: rest item 0-2 (needed), favourite-flavour food 0-2 (probably needed), toys and decorations 0-6 combined, liked environment 0 or 2 (optional); a total of 9 or more gives Awesome, 6-8 Great, 4-5 Nice, 0-3 Average. Feeding a favourite food for 7 days alone only reached Nice. Blocking the path to the Pokémon Center did not change comfort in this test — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- [community-tested] ぽこポケ学会's order of comfort factors (roughly by importance): no disliked environment within range; liked environment met; not hungry; rest, toy and decoration all in range; some favourite items in range (duplicates add little or nothing); kit house > built house > habitat (hypothesis); path to the Pokémon Center connected. The "すみごこちどう？" request comes once a day — [ぽこポケ学会](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/)
- [official patch notes via guide] Version 2.0.0 (5 August 2026): a Pokémon whose habitat is destroyed moves back in if the same habitat is rebuilt nearby; Pokémon made sad by removed furniture notice its replacement more quickly — [Serebii, Patches & Updates](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/patch.shtml)
- [guide] GameWith: the level drops when you take a Pokémon to another map (the higher its comfort, the bigger the drop) and when you remove a habitat or nearby furniture; rebuilding the Pokémon Center and levelling the road to it "raise it a lot"; doing "すみごこちどう？" each day (once per Pokémon per day; changing the date works) alone can reach Lv. 5; max is 10 — [GameWith](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/545345)

### Inferences
- A planner can encode: score = sum over residents of {Awesome 10, Great 5, Nice 2, Average 1, Iffy 0, no home 0}, x2 for Legendary/Mythical; level = highest L with score >= T[L], T = {1:0, 2:3, 3:12, 4:26, 5:52, 6:77, 7:100, 8:120, 9:140, 10:160}. Lv. 2 = 3 is the shakiest value (estimated from a meter, not measured at the boundary). Treat downward moves as lagging (keep the level until roughly 75% of the way back into the band, or about 2 points under the threshold at Lv. 5).
- Under that model, "requests", "rebuilding the Pokémon Center", "kits/buildings" and "paving the road" (which guides list as separate sources) most likely work through residents' comfort rather than as separate point sources; no test shows a flat bonus from them. Serebii's line that kits help "even if a Pokémon doesn't immediately live there" is the only claim of a non-resident source and is untested.
- Minimum Lv. 10 recipes: 16 Awesome regular residents, or 8 Awesome Legendaries, or mixes such as 8 Awesome + 16 Great (PokoTraqr's example as quoted in snippets).
- Ditto's own house counts like a resident (ぽこポケ学会, and PokoTraqr says Ditto scores like a regular Pokémon), so the player's house is a planner-relevant comfort source.
- Moving a Pokémon to another town removes its points from the old town at once (subject to the hysteresis) and adds them to the new one once it settles and notices its surroundings.

### Gaps
- No official (Nintendo/in-game text) or datamined point table or threshold list was found; both tested sources are fan measurements from version 1.0.x (March 2026). Whether version 1.1.0 or 2.0.0 changed the values was not tested by anyone found.
- The exact downward hysteresis rule (75% of band per PokoTraqr vs "about 2 points" observed by ぼってぃ) is not pinned down; these may be the same rule seen at different levels.
- Whether story-special residents (Peakychu, Chef Dente, Tinkmaster, DJ Rotom, Mosslax, Professor Tangrowth) and Hoppip-line event Pokémon count: PokoTraqr reportedly says story Pokémon and Hoppip add nothing (search snippet quoted in the sibling notes file residents_and_comfort.md); unconfirmed here.
- Whether Cloud Island and Palette Town use the same thresholds was not tested; GameWith's shop tables show both run to Lv. 10.
- The Mogsetsu Reddit thread itself could not be opened (reddit blocked; search budget spent), so its sample sizes are unknown.

## What each environment level unlocks per area

### Takeaway
Every town (including Palette Town, Bubbly Basin and each Cloud Island) has its own 10-level ladder that adds shop stock (items, recipes, recipe sets, house kits, habitat hints, materials), PC challenges and resident requests. Fixed story gates sit at Lv. 2 (Withered Wastelands: multiplayer and daily challenges), Lv. 3 (Pokémon Center kit and the "Fix the Pokémon Center" request in every town; Bubbly Basin's temple story) and Lv. 5 (the "さらに環境レベルを上げよう！" reward item needed for the ending; Bleak Beach Lv. 5 also gates Dive and Bubbly Basin). Serebii and GameWith publish full per-level shop lists that mostly agree but differ on about 15 placements.

### Inferences
- For the planner the shop ladder is the single biggest unlock source: house kits come in a fixed pattern per story town (room kit Lv. 2, hut Lv. 3, house Lv. 4, grand kit Lv. 5, coloured hut Lv. 4 or 5, a special landmark kit at Lv. 8, the town's Pokémon Center kit at Lv. 10, mid and top kits unlimited at Lv. 9/10). Pokémetal at Lv. 6 and Rare Pokémetal at Lv. 7 are the same in every story town.
- Where Serebii and GameWith disagree, GameWith's page is newer (updated 2026-11-23) and agrees with data.json on several entries Serebii lacks (House partition small/medium and Wooden beam at Withered Wastelands Lv. 5, Stylish steps at Palette Town Lv. 4, Music box at Bubbly Basin Lv. 5), so GameWith looks like the better tiebreaker; Serebii's placing of the Wasteland Pokémon Center kit and Inflatable boat under Bleak Beach looks like a table slip.
- Because a level drop re-locks unpurchased stock (not purchased items), a planner should treat shop unlocks as "available while level >= N".

### Gaps
- Lv. 5 and Lv. 10 "special gifts" (Serebii) were only named for Lv. 5 (Cracker / Washing machine / Refrigerator / Game Boy via the request); the Lv. 10 recipe gift contents per town were not found.
- PC challenge lists per level (and their Life Coin rewards) were not collected.
- Which resident requests unlock at which level is not tabulated anywhere found.
- Cloud Island ladder: Serebii lists 159 items over Lv. 1-10 (all purchases stay on the island); the sibling file islands_multiplayer_updates.md covers Cloud Islands in more depth.

## Story order and area unlocks; post-game

### Takeaway

## Ground restoration

### Takeaway
No source found says restored ground is scored on its own; environment level tracks residents' comfort. Restoring dry ground (Water Gun on dry grass, trees and flowers; Leafage to grow grass) is a story and habitat tool: it spawns the first Pokémon, feeds humidity for Withered Wastelands' rain story, and builds habitats, which then feed comfort.

### Cited Findings
- [guide] Opening sequence: Water Gun on a 2x2 dry grass patch makes it sway and Bulbasaur appears; Bulbasaur's first request is watering 10 dry grass tiles, which teaches Leafage; Water Gun also revives dead flowers and trees (dead trees give PP-restoring berries when revived) — [Game8 JP story chart](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767282)
- [guide] Yawn Up A Storm: raise Withered Wastelands humidity to 100 with Water Gun on water, rivers opened by breaking rocks, and flower patches; then Kyogre's rain "moistens up the entire surrounding area" — [Serebii Important Requests](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/importantrequests.shtml)
- [community-tested, single test] Blocking the path to the Pokémon Center did not lower a resident's comfort; the tester suggests the condition may be "no dry or cracked ground" rather than physical access — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- [guide] Serebii lists dry and barren terrain among Withered Wastelands' natural blocks (Dry tall grass, Dry Leppa tree, Dry field grass, Barren ordinary soil, Dry wildflowers) and Palette Town's (Dry tall grass, Dry wildflowers, Dry field grass, Barren ordinary soil, Dry Leppa tree); Sparkling Skylands has Barren pure-white soil and dry shoots — [Serebii Withered Wastelands](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/locations/witheredwastelands.shtml); [Serebii Palette Town](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/locations/palettetown.shtml); [Serebii Sparkling Skylands](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/locations/sparklingskylands.shtml)
- [official patch notes via guide] Version 2.0.0: Leafage on farm soil reverts it to a normal soil block — [Serebii patch](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/patch.shtml)

### Inferences
- For the planner, model restoration as terrain state that habitats and humidity need, not as environment-level points. Dry variants (barren soil, dry grass, dry trees) mainly occur in Withered Wastelands and Palette Town.
- ぼってぃ's hypothesis that the "path to the Pokémon Center" condition may really mean "no dry or cracked ground on the way" is untested and would be the only scored link between ground state and comfort.

### Gaps
- No test found of whether converting large areas of dry ground changes environment level directly; one targeted web search was planned but the session's search budget was used up.
- Per-area terrain specifics (Skylands high ground, Bleak Beach ocean, Rocky Ridges lava) as build rules are covered by the sibling notes on height/stacking and liquids, not here.

## Area-specific rules; town vs area

### Takeaway
The game calls each map a 街 (town); "area" in English guides means the same thing. Each town has its own environment level, shop ladder, Pokémon Center, natural materials and exclusive Pokémon, and story towns are themed on Kanto cities. Palette Town is the only town where multiplayer guests can build and the only GameShare town; other towns are visit-only for guests. Map height is 97 blocks up and 30 down from sea level; horizontal size differs by map (fan estimates 220 to 380 blocks).

### Cited Findings
- [guide] Story towns map onto old Kanto: Withered Wastelands = Fuchsia City and surroundings (task: make it rain); Bleak Beach = Vermilion City plus the S.S. - [guide] Palette Town exclusive Pokémon (Serebii): Geodude, Scizor, Skwovet, Machop, Cramorant, Magby, Vikavolt, Rowlet, Pikachu, Tinkatink, Tinkatuff, Munchlax, Snorlax, Weezing, Tangela, Koffing, Mawile, and Eevee's evolutions Vaporeon, Jolteon, Flareon, Espeon, Umbreon, Leafeon, Glaceon, Sylveon — [Serebii Palette Town](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/locations/palettetown.shtml)

### Gaps
- Exact horizontal map sizes per town are unmeasured; the 256x256 figure is one player's guess from the minimap.
- Full per-town Pokémon pools were not compiled here (Serebii location pages list exclusive Pokémon; the sibling habitats notes cover spawning).
- Build limits per town are covered by earlier research (per-area item caps) and were skipped.

## Town-wide score, rank or rating

### Takeaway
There is no single town-wide score in the UI found by guides; the player-wide Trainer Rank (Super, Hyper, Master) comes from story requests and opens gates. GameWith's own test suggests the sum of all towns' environment levels raises Legendary odds on Dream Islands.

### Cited Findings
- [guide] The only progression rank beyond per-town environment level is the player's Trainer Rank (トレーナーランク), raised by clearing story important requests (Super, Hyper, Master); its effect is opening area gates. Serebii: "With each completion, your Trainer Rank will increased further" — [Game8 JP トレーナーランク](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/768203); [Serebii Important Requests](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/importantrequests.shtml)
