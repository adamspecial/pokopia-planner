## 1. From wild Pokémon to resident (move-in, choosing a home, what blocks it)

### Takeaway
(pending)

### Cited Findings
- **[Guide, Game8 JP, updated 2026-11-23]** Moving a Pokémon's home (すみか): (1) build a new home where you want it; it does **not** need to be the habitat the Pokémon spawns from (example: Bulbasaur spawns from green tall grass, but its new home need not be green tall grass); (2) talk to the Pokémon and pick 「いっしょに行こう」 (come with me); (3) go near the new home (the top-left "ちかくの生息地" (nearby habitat) panel appears); (4) talk to it and pick 「ここにおひっこしする？」 (move here?); (5) its home changes and it is added to the nearby-habitat panel. "新しいすみかに既にポケモンがいる場合はおひっこしはできません" (you can't move it into a new home that already has a Pokémon; said of habitats). — [Game8 JP: すみかの移動方法と引っ越しのやり方](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767983)
- **[Guide, Game8 JP]** A move is **refused** if the destination contains something the Pokémon dislikes ("引っ越し先のすみかにポケモンの苦手なものがあると引っ越しを拒否されます"). Example given: Charmander cannot move into a place with water. — [Game8 JP: 引っ越し](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767983)
- **[Guide, Game8 JP]** Pokémon sometimes ask to move on their own ("ポケモンが自ら引っ越したいと申し出てくる場合もあります"). A house has a fixed number of residents, and **a move-in is impossible unless 3 or more kinds of furniture are placed** ("家具を3種類以上設置しないと引っ越しができない"). — [Game8 JP: すみごこちの上げ方とメリット](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767513)
- **[Community-tested]** House conditions (ぽこポケ学会): an enclosed space with at least 1 door; walls 1 high are enough; 2-cell doors OK; floor area 2×2 or more (1×4 not allowed); 9×10 or more not allowed; a block directly in front of the door (outside) cancels it; "(引っ越し時のみ)3種類の異なる家具が存在する" (3 different furniture kinds, **only at move-in**). Up to 4 Pokémon of any size per block house. A habitat built inside a house also creates its own dwelling (a 2×2 house with the Psychic-power habitat (simple cushion + crystal ball) and the photo habitat (tablet + party cup) inside holds 4 + 2 Pokémon). — [ぽこポケ学会: 家と住み心地](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/)

- **[Guide, 攻略大百科, updated 2026-09-01]** Moving into a house: lead it with 「いっしょに行こう」, go inside the house, pick 「ここに おひっこしする？」. "家具が3種類必要 … 3種類の家具がないと「ここに おひっこしする？」を選択しても断られます" (without 3 kinds the Pokémon refuses). "小さなポケモンが大きな家に住むことはできますが、リザードンやスイクンなどの大きいポケモンは、小さな家に住むことができません" (small Pokémon can live in big houses, but big ones such as Charizard and Suicune can't live in small houses). — [攻略大百科: すみごこち](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/3509)
- **[Guide, 攻略大百科]** Habitat occupancy: "すでに別のポケモンが住んでいる生息地には、新しいポケモンがやってくることはありません" (a habitat that already has a resident attracts no new Pokémon); to get another Pokémon from it, move the resident to an empty habitat. Each species appears only once ("同じポケモンは1匹しか出現しません"; "同じポケモンは1体しか入手できません"), so with duplicate habitats the Pokémon may be heading for a different copy. Rarer Pokémon (★1–★3 めずらしさ) take longer to appear. Habitats placed close together can stop the wanted one from spawning. Success shows a sparkle effect and the habitat appears under 「ちかくの生息地」 top-left. — [攻略大百科: ポケモンが来ないときの対策](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/3417)
- **[Guide, Game8 EN]** The general request "Build a House" (all areas): "Build a house and put three different pieces of furniture inside." — [Game8: List of All Requests](https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Pokopia/archives/583451)
- **[Guide, Game8 JP]** Ditto's own home (the house with Ditto's flag) can take residents: the Palette Town ("まっさらな街") special challenge 「ポケモンと住もう」 is cleared by moving any Pokémon into it (kit or block house both fine); reward: afternoon tea set (a habitat item). — [Game8 JP: ポケモンと住もう](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/769154)

## 2. Home capacities and housemates

### Takeaway
(pending)

### Cited Findings
- **[Guide, Game8 JP]** Kit-house capacity is set by size: small 1, medium 2, large 4 ("小は1匹、中は2匹、大は4匹"); the size is shown in the kit's name/description. Maximum 4 in one house. — [Game8 JP: 引っ越し](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767983)
- **[Community-tested]** Block houses hold up to 4 regardless of Pokémon size ("ポケモンの大きさを問わず、1つの家に4匹まで"). — [ぽこポケ学会](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/)
- **[Guide, Game8 JP]** Shared ranges: placing homes next to each other overlaps their comfort ranges; furniture in the overlap counts for both, and Pokémon with the same favourites can be served by one item. — [Game8 JP: 引っ越し](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767983)

- **[Guide, Game8 JP, 改築 page]** "「〇〇のおへや」「〇〇のこや」シリーズは1匹までで、「〇〇のいえ」シリーズだと2匹まで … ブロックで作った家の場合は、サイズに関係なく4匹まで" (den-type おへや and hut-type こや kits hold 1, cottage-type いえ kits hold 2; block houses hold 4 whatever their size). Kit houses can only be remodelled (改築) into a **larger** kit placed in front; furniture carries over; shared materials are discounted; not while a Pokémon is inside or while it is Ditto's home (remove the flag); block houses can't be remodelled, only extended by hand. A bigger house raises its residents' comfort more. — [Game8 JP: 改築のやり方](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/769081)
- **[Guide, 攻略大百科]** りっぱな (grand) kit houses are roomy, hold several Pokémon and lots of furniture, so comfort rises very easily. — [攻略大百科: すみごこち](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/3509)
- **[Guide, fan tools, search summaries only]** Fan roommate calculators (PokeTools Roommate/Housing Calculator, PokoTraqr, Pokopia Companion, PokoMatch, pokopiamap Roommate Matchmaker) score housemates by shared ideal habitat, overlap of the 5 favourite categories, type and specialty. One guide claims housemates "must share the same ideal habitat type … to live together comfortably"; the pokopiamap matchmaker says it **does not verify an in-game roommate rule**. None cites a test. — [PokeTools Roommate Calculator](https://www.poketools.com/pokopia-roommate-calculator); [PokoTraqr Housing Guide](https://www.pokotraqr.com/guides/housing-guide); [Pokopia Companion: Roommates](https://www.pokopiacompanion.com/homes/roommates); [pokopiamap Roommate Matchmaker](https://pokopiamap.com/roommate-matchmaker)

## 3. Comfort (すみごこち): inputs, levels, thresholds, display

### Takeaway
(pending)

### Cited Findings
- **[Guide, Serebii]** English names of the 5 comfort levels with a home: **Awesome, Great, Nice, Average, Iffy** (= 最高, めちゃイイ感じ, ちょっといい感じ, ふつう, びみょう). "First, a Pokémon needs to have a habitat or home to live in. If they don't have one, then their Comfy Level is seen as 0." Asking about comfort gives a specific desire (food, or a decoration for the habitat); fulfilling it raises the level, and continuing to give gifts daily and talking raises it further. A matching ideal habitat (Warm, Humid, Dark, ...) raises comfort; disliked things near the habitat lower it. "If a Pokémon is in a built house rather than just a habitat, their Comfy Level will increase quicker." Since Bubbly Basin (Ver.2.0.0), a following Pokémon with the Scrub specialty cleans Pokémon you meet, boosting their comfort and friendship. — [Serebii: Environment & Comfy Level](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/environmentlevel.shtml)
- **[Guide, Serebii]** Favourite items: placing items that match a Pokémon's favourites in its home or habitat raises its Comfy Level "at a higher rate"; the game shows this as **a bigger sparkle** when the item is put down in their home. — [Serebii: Favorites](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/favorites.shtml)
- **[Official patch note, Ver.2.0.0, 2026-08-05]** "Pokémon that are sad because furniture was removed will now notice more quickly when the same furniture is replaced." (So removing furniture makes a Pokémon sad, i.e. lowers comfort.) — [Serebii: Patches & Updates](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/patch.shtml)
- **[Guide, 攻略大百科]** Comfort (unlike friendship) can fall when you use furniture, toys or food that the Pokémon doesn't like. — [攻略大百科: 仲良し度のあげ方](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/4899)
- **[Community-tested, ぼってぃ note, 2026-03-16]** Comfort ranks seen in game, lowest to highest: すみかなし (no home) < びみょう (meh) < ふつう (okay) < ちょっといい感じ (a bit nice) < めちゃイイ感じ (really nice) < 最高 (best). — [ぼってぃ: 環境レベルを検証する](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Community-tested, ぼってぃ]** Environment-Level points per Pokémon by comfort rank (solved from two towns with identical meters: Rugged Mountain 4 best / 5 really nice / 13 a bit nice / 2 okay, Sparkling Skylands 2 / 8 / 15 / 3, both totalling 93): **最高 10, めちゃイイ感じ 5, ちょっといい感じ 2, ふつう 1, びみょう 0, すみかなし 0**. ("すみごこちpt" is the tester's own name.) — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Community-tested, ぼってぃ]** Cumulative points needed per Environment Level: Lv2 3, Lv3 12, Lv4 26, Lv5 52, Lv6 77, Lv7 100, Lv8 120, Lv9 140, Lv10 160. So 16 Pokémon at 最高 reach Lv10. When points drop, the level is held with some slack (e.g. Lv5 kept down to about 50 pts after falling below 52). — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Community-tested hypothesis, ぼってぃ]** Comfort as a sum of factor scores (0 = none, 1 = present, 2 = a favourite): rest item 0–2 (probably required), favourite-flavour food given 0–2 (probably required), toys 0–4 (up to 2?), decorations 0–4 (up to 2?), environment 0 or 2 (not required). Observed mapping: **9+ = 最高, 6–8 = めちゃイイ感じ, 4–5 = ちょっといい感じ, 0–3 = ふつう, びみょう = negative?** Worked example (Lotad line): food fav 2 + environment humid 2 + rest "nature" 2 + toy "noisy" 2 + decoration "containers" 2 = 10 → 最高. The tester calls the mapping "完全に体感の域を出ない" (purely a feel). A later variant: toy + decoration 0–6, with one toy and one decoration certain and a third of either. — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Community-tested, ぼってぃ]** Feeding only the favourite-flavour food for 7 days in a row (sour Aspear Berry to Machoke) never got above ちょっといい感じ. Placing non-favourite items only also caps at ちょっといい感じ. Items not named in the "how's your comfort?" answer still raise comfort once the Pokémon notices them. — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Community-tested, ぼってぃ]** Comfort updates only when the Pokémon **notices** the item (it says 「見てみるよ」 then 「ありがとう」); off-screen Pokémon don't notice. Dismissing a party Pokémon in front of the item, or reloading the area, triggers noticing. A home assigned from "no home" reached 最高 in one day when all conditions were met. Possible ceiling: Pokémon may climb fast to their previous best but need days (up to 3?) to go above it (unverified). — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Community-tested, ぼってぃ]** Downward stickiness: after a Pokémon had reached ふつう, moving it to a bright spot, blocking the path to the Pokémon Center, lighting a dark-lover's room and walling a home in ice did **not** drop it below ふつう; a fresh Pokémon given a new home started at びみょう (Swirlix). — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Official, in-game hints as quoted by ぼってぃ]** The in-game "くらしのヒント" (life tips) list plus factors: favourite environment, rest furniture, giving food, toys, decorations, a built house, and legendary/mythical Pokémon having a large effect on the level; minus factors: disliked things, an unpaved path to the Pokémon Center. — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Community-tested, ぽこポケ学会]** Factors for 最高, roughly most important first ("未解明な部分が多い"): (1) no disliked environment within range (e.g. fire near a Grass Pokémon); (2) favourite environment met; (3) not hungry; (4) rest / toy / decoration (きゅうけい/おもちゃ/かざりつけ) all in range; (5) some favourites in range ("同じ家具は加算されない or 効果が著しく薄れる？": the same furniture doesn't add, or adds much less?); (hypothesis) kit house > block house > habitat; a road connected to the Pokémon Center. — [ぽこポケ学会](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/)
- **[Community-tested, ぽこポケ学会]** 「すみごこちどう？」 gives one wish per day; a hungry Pokémon asks for food first, so give food before asking. New furniture has no effect until the Pokémon notices it; if a Pokémon names no wish, time passing or changing area often turns it to 最高. — [ぽこポケ学会](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/)
- **[Community-tested, ぽこポケ学会]** Ditto's (player) house: comfort maxes at **13 kinds**; toy, rest and decoration each count up to **5 kinds** only; it affects the Environment Level. — [ぽこポケ学会](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/)
- **[Guide, Game8 JP]** Display: open the PC and press the − button to see every Pokémon's current comfort; or talk to a Pokémon and pick 「すみごこち どう？」. Solving the asked-for wish raises comfort **one rank** (e.g. びみょう → ふつう), and only one wish is learnt per day. Kit houses, especially the shop's 「◯◯のりっぱなキット」 houses, raise comfort more easily than small houses or block houses. — [Game8 JP: すみごこち](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767513)
- **[Guide, Game8 JP]** "家に住んでいるポケモンのすみごこちは、家の外の環境を整えても変化しなくなります" (a Pokémon living in a house is no longer affected by outdoor environment changes); "住んでいる家が大きいほど、すみごこちも上がりやすい" (the bigger the house, the easier comfort rises). — [Game8 JP: 引っ越し](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767983)

- **[Guide, 攻略大百科]** Comfort ranks: 6 in all — 最高, めちゃイイ感じ, ちょっといい感じ, ふつう, びみょう, すみかなし. Check them all at the PC in front of the Pokémon Center with the − button (only the current area's Pokémon). The daily 「すみごこち どう？」 improvement is once per real-time day; the game day changes at **5:00 AM**. Pressing the right stick near a home shows its range; put items where several homes' ranges overlap. Dark-loving Pokémon are easier to satisfy in a house. Item tags (おもちゃ toy, かざりつけ decoration, きゅうけい rest) are shown in the item details. **Pokémon can't use a bed or chair smaller than themselves** ("自身より小さいベッドやイスを使うことはできません"). Disliked things near the home lower comfort: fire items (campfire) near Grass types like Bulbasaur, water items (Horsea fountain) near Fire types like Charmander. Paving roads and adding stairs to the Pokémon Center helps. — [攻略大百科: すみごこち](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/3509)
- **[Guide, Game8 JP]** Paving the road to the Pokémon Center (fill holes, add stairs at height differences) makes Pokémon go out more easily and raises comfort. — [Game8 JP: 環境レベル](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767551); ぼってぃ's single test of blocking the path saw no change. — [ぼってぃ](https://note.com/bottical/n/nee0a7bbcf440)
- **[Community research via fan calculator, search summary only]** PokoTraqr's Environment Level Calculator credits u/mogsetsu's r/Pokopia research: regular Pokémon score **10 Awesome / 5 Great / 2 Nice / 1 Average**, **legendaries score double**, Iffy and "Exploring" (no home) score 0; story Pokémon and Hoppip add nothing; a level only drops once you fall below about 75 % of that level's progress; fastest Lv10 = 8 Awesome (80) + 16 Great (80) = 160 pts. Matches ぼってぃ's independent figures. A different calculator (Pokopia Island) uses 0–8 points per Pokémon ÷ 8, +5 per legendary, +1 per 3 habitat types; no test cited. — [PokoTraqr calculator](https://www.pokotraqr.com/paradise/env-calculator); [Pokopia Island calculator](https://pokopiaisland.com/tools/environment-calculator) (search summaries)
- **[Unverified]** pokopia.center claims comfort caps at 6 seating pieces per room. No test; looks like generated guide text. — [pokopia.center](https://pokopia.center/posts/pokopia-furniture-categories-complete-guide-2026/) (search summary)

## 4. What comfort changes

### Takeaway
(pending)

### Cited Findings
- **[Guide, Serebii]** Environment Level runs 1–10 per area. Each level adds Poké Life PC challenges and shop items (different per area); Lv5 gives a gift of items and Lv10 gives recipes. Requests such as restoring the Pokémon Center also raise it, and "If you add buildings and other kits, it will also help raise it even if a Pokémon doesn't immediately live there." A legendary or mythical Pokémon with raised comfort makes it rise quicker. Removing Pokémon from an area can lower the level, which re-locks unpurchased shop items and challenges of that level. — [Serebii: Environment & Comfy Level](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/environmentlevel.shtml)
- **[Guide, Game8 JP]** The Environment Level of a town is the sum of its Pokémon's comfort; higher levels add PC challenges (the only source of Life Coins), shop stock (furniture, recipes, bag upgrades, Point Up, Pokémon Center rebuild kit from Lv3) and new requests. A falling level removes the added shop stock until it rises again. Taking Pokémon to another map changes the sum and can drop the level; broken comfort items and fires put out by rain lower comfort. — [Game8 JP: 環境レベル](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767551)
- **[Guide, Game8 JP]** Legendary and mythical Pokémon met on Dream Islands raise the Environment Level more than others. — [Game8 JP: 環境レベル](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767551)

- **[Guide, Serebii]** Litter specialty: "The higher the Pokémon comfort level, the more items that get brought." — [Serebii: Litter](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/litter.shtml). The Games Wiki (search summary only) says Awesome gives the highest Litter drop rate. — [The Games Wiki: Litter](https://thegameswiki.com/pokopia/wiki/litter-specialty-and-item-drops)
- **[Guide, Game8 EN]** Completing requests raises the requester's comfort level. — [Game8: List of All Requests](https://game8.co/games/Pokemon-Pokopia/archives/583451)

## 5. Friendship (親密度 / なかよし)

### Takeaway
(pending)

### Cited Findings
- **[Guide, Serebii]** Friendship is separate from comfort, "though their friendship can affect the growth of that as well". Completing a Pokémon's request raises its friendship gauge; giving a gift raises it. As it rises the Pokémon is happy to see you and sometimes gives gifts of materials it found; near the top it calls you by your name instead of "Ditto". Pokémon periodically invite you to a game (a quiz about a Pokémon it is friends with, or Look This Way, best of 3 rounds): playing always gives a boost, winning gives more. At maximum it announces it is your **Best Friend**, is always happy to see you, and its Pokédex entry gets a special mark. — [Serebii: Friendship](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/friendship.shtml)
- **[Guide, Serebii]** Giving an item that matches one of the Pokémon's favourites raises friendship "at a higher level". — [Serebii: Favorites](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/favorites.shtml)
- **[Community-tested by the guide's team, 攻略大百科]** Friendship (仲良し度) is hidden: no number. Signs: the Pokémon switches from calling you "メタモン" to your player name; it starts giving presents; special conversations (only for some Pokémon); once maxed, friendship-raising actions no longer show the friendship heart; the Pokédex page shows a **heart mark** at max. — [攻略大百科: 仲良し度のあげ方](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/4899)
- **[Community-tested, 攻略大百科]** Does it fall? Unknown officially; after leaving Pokémon alone for a long time the heart stayed and they kept using the player's name. — [攻略大百科](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/4899)
- **[Community-tested, 攻略大百科]** Gift limit: friendship rises from a present **once per day** per Pokémon; extra rises come from accepting a random play invitation or talking to a Pokémon with a 「・・・」 bubble. Winning or losing a game doesn't matter (contradicts Serebii's "winning gives more"). — [攻略大百科](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/4899); contradicted by [Serebii: Friendship](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/friendship.shtml)
- **[Community-tested, 攻略大百科]** Number of favourite-flavour **berries** to reach max friendship, by comfort: びみょう or すみかなし **8**; just appeared from a new habitat **7**; ちょっといい感じ **6**; ふつう after many days **6 + one Look This Way game**. Lower comfort tends to need more gifts. Favourite cooked dishes or favourite vegetables can cut the count further. Plain-flavour food (Leppa Berry ヒメリのみ, fresh water) also raises friendship but takes much longer. — [攻略大百科](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/4899)
- **[Guide, 攻略大百科]** Smaller friendship gains: leading it with 「一緒に行こう！」, cooking together, having it help build, having it help with its specialty. — [攻略大百科](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/4899)
- **[Guide, 攻略大百科]** Rewards: presents (hedge seeds, vegetables, iron and other materials); friendship-raising actions also raise comfort, separately from the daily wish; higher friendship raises the chance a Pokémon teaches an emote (e.g. Piplup ドヤッ!, Riolu high five, Scorbunny うんうん, Charmander thank you, Trubbish ガックリ, Empoleon, Politoed, Ludicolo, Rowlet, Toxel, Meowscarada, Meowth, Gengar ばあっ!). — [攻略大百科](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/4899)
- **[Guide, Game8 JP]** Food has 6 flavours: あまい sweet, からい spicy, にがい bitter, しぶい dry, すっぱい sour, クセがない plain. Giving a Pokémon food of its favourite flavour raises **親密度** (friendship) more than usual. **No Pokémon has "plain" as its favourite**, so plain foods (ヒメリのみ Leppa Berry, plain salad, fresh water, colourful hamburger) are the ones to spend first. — [Game8 JP: 味の種類と食べ物の一覧](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/771031)

## 6. Requests (おねがいごと)

### Takeaway
(pending)

### Cited Findings
- **[Guide, Serebii]** Completing a Pokémon's request raises its friendship gauge. — [Serebii: Friendship](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/friendship.shtml)
- **[Official patch note, Ver.2.0.0]** Listed under **Fixes** (so fixed from 2.0.0): "Requests from Pokémon may occasionally ask for items that the requesting Pokémon actually dislikes." Ver.2.0.0 also "Reduced ordinary conversation indicators so that special Pokémon conversations and requests directed at Ditto stand out more clearly." — [Serebii: Patches & Updates](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/patch.shtml)
- **[Guide, Game8 JP]** Pokémon with a request show an icon over their head. Each Environment Level unlocks new requests from the town's Pokémon; environment-preference requests ("too bright", "too dark") are easy and raise comfort when done. — [Game8 JP: 環境レベル](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767551)

- **[Official patch note, Ver.1.0.3, 2026-04-09]** Fixed: couldn't find Pokémon without habitats with the Search function; impossible to move habitats. — [Serebii: Patches & Updates](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/patch.shtml)
- **[Guide, Game8 JP]** Moving the player's own house: buy 「ひっこしキット・じゅんび」 at the PC shop, place it in front of the house, bring the asked Pokémon (only the specialty matters, e.g. one Bulldoze Pokémon plus any one Pokémon), collect 「ひっこしキット・たてかえ」 after the wait, place it at the new spot and build; furniture and resident data carry over. The moving kit works only for kit houses built from 「◯◯のいえキット」; **not** for block houses or habitats, whose residents must be led to a new home by hand. It cannot start while a Pokémon is inside, or while Ditto's flag is attached outside. — [Game8 JP: 引っ越し](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767983)

- **[Guide, Game8 JP, 模様替え page]** When rearranging, don't let a house drop **below 3 furniture**: "3つ未満になると家として判定されなくなってしまうので、ポケモンたちを住ませていた場合はすみかがなくなってしまいます" (below 3 it stops being a house and its residents lose their home; you must bring them back and move them in again). This **contradicts** ぽこポケ学会's "(引っ越し時のみ)" (3 kinds checked only at move-in). — [Game8 JP: 模様替え](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/769142); contradicted by [ぽこポケ学会](https://kyu49.github.io/Pokopia/house/)
- **[Guide, Game8 JP]** A block house is too big if its floor exceeds 10×9 and stops counting as a house. — [Game8 JP: 模様替え](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/769142)
- **[Guide, 攻略大百科]** すみかなし (homeless) Pokémon stay in the area. If the area has **25 or fewer** Pokémon they wander somewhere in it; above 25 they may not appear. Fix: rebuild the matching habitat (or the same one) near where it lived and wait; using Honey (あまいミツ) at the rebuilt habitat can call it back without waiting a day. Homeless Pokémon can't be found with the Pokédex search. — [攻略大百科: ポケモンが来ないときの対策](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/3417)
- **[Guide, 攻略大百科]** Some Pokémon can't be led out of their area until their sub-quest or the story/event finishes (e.g. Piplup during 「滝を作ろう」). — [攻略大百科](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/3417)
