## 1. Every move Ditto learns: teacher, unlock, effects, area, upgrades

### Takeaway
Ditto has 3 built-in actions (Headbutt, Stockpile/Spit Up for carrying and placing items) and 15 learned moves. Withered Wastelands teaches Water Gun, Leafage, Cut, Rock Smash, Rototiller and Splash. Bleak Beach teaches Surf, Stockpile Water and Camouflage, plus Dive from 2.0.0. Rocky Ridges teaches Strength and Rollout. Sparkling Skylands teaches Glide and Waterfall. The post-game Magnet Rise comes from Withered Wastelands. Each move is unlocked by one Pokémon's request. **There are no permanent move levels.** The only "upgrade" is a temporary food buff: salad (Leafage), bread (Cut), hamburger steak (Rock Smash and Rollout), soup (Water Gun), and from 2.0.0 smoothie (Surf). Each buff runs on its own yellow PP gauge. Guide tile areas: Water Gun is a 5-tile plus, powered "+2 orthogonal, +1 diagonal". Leafage is 1 tile, powered a plus. Rototiller is 1 tile per press. Cut is 1 tile, powered a forward slash (earlier research: 3×6). Rock Smash is 1 block per punch and repeats while held.

- **Cut, normal (guide, Game8 JP):** cuts in front; "the range is not wide, so you need to use it many times" for a big patch. Cuts grass, trees, vines, cloth and wooden objects (barrels, chairs); does almost nothing to stone or iron — [Game8 JP いあいぎり](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767900)
- **Cut, powered:** held, fires a flying slash with longer reach. Earlier research recorded powered Cut as cutting 3×6 tiles (Game8 automation page) — [Game8 JP いあいぎり](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767900); [Game8 JP 自動化](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/769942)
- **Rototiller (guide, Game8 JP):** tills "only the one tile in front" (一度に耕せるのは目の前の1マスのみ) and keeps tilling while held. Combine with ZL strafing (並行移動) to till lines. Used on grass or flowers it lifts them so they can be carried, replanted, gifted or bagged. Dry (干からびた) soil can't be tilled until watered — [Game8 JP たがやす](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/768196)


**More per-move detail (guide, Game8 JP pages, each "覚え方と効果・使い方")**
- **Rock Smash (いわくだき):** a punch at the block in front. Some blocks are too tough to break in one hit. Holding the button repeats punches; turning up or down while repeating hits the block overhead or underfoot. Powered (hamburger steak, ハンバーグ, made in a frying pan from beans) it breaks "gold, iron, PokéMetal" and other things it normally can't. **Colourful steak (いろどりハンバーグ: beans + salad + potato + free) and potato steak (ジャガイモハンバーグ: beans + potato + 2 free)** power it up more than other steaks and break hard blocks faster. Powered Rock Smash also powers Rollout — [Game8 JP いわくだき](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/768193)
- **Rock Smash (guide, GameWith):** breaks the block in front ("選択範囲のブロック"). Hold ZR to repeat while walking. The target block is chosen with the right stick, or with mouse mode, which can pick non-adjacent blocks anywhere on screen. **Blocks that only powered Rock Smash breaks (8):** black rock (くろいいわ), iron (てつ), gold (きん), large stone (おおきないし), magma rock (マグマいわ), hot rock (アツアツいわ), light stone (ヒカリいし), PokéMetal (ポケメタル), mostly found in Rocky Ridges and Sparkling Skylands — [GameWith いわくだき](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/547901)
- **Rollout (ころがる):** ZR turns Ditto into Graveler; B (default) cancels. Pressing the move button again while rolling breaks what you hit; holding it keeps breaking as you move. Dash makes it much faster than running. It **drains PP continuously while transformed**. It breaks only what Rock Smash can break (powered Rock Smash widens this). With Splash learned you can jump while rolling — [Game8 JP ころがる](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/768259). **Rollout cannot break blocks lower than itself (underfoot)**, so levelling starts one tile below the highest point — [Game8 JP 整地](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/768699). Ver. 2.0.0: it also breaks some natural objects such as large boulders — [Serebii patches](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/patch.shtml)
- **Strength (かいりき):** hold A in front of a heavy object to push or pull it; tilt the L stick left/right to rotate it. Moves stones, hard rocks, worn lumber, storage boxes, workbenches, signs. **Horizontal only; it cannot lift objects up or down** (Magnet Rise can) — [Game8 JP かいりき](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/768258)
- **Surf (なみのり):** B in water turns Ditto into Lapras. Without it, Ditto cannot swim and is pushed back to land on entering deep water. If PP runs out while surfing, Surf continues until you leave the form. Powered by smoothie (DLC1 story): transform with B even deep underwater, move fast underwater, and break underwater blocks with ZR (holdable) — [Game8 JP なみのり](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/768269)
- **Camouflage / Mimic (ものまね, ZR):** become a look-alike of the object in front, from something as small as a leaf to the size of a streetlight; not houses or anything much bigger than Ditto. You can move (and jump, with Splash) while transformed. Game8: it has no use except the Hide-and-Sneak minigame (かくれおに). Zorua is disguised as a block in front of the Pokémon Center once "Brighten the town" starts — [Game8 JP ものまね](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/768271)
- **Stockpile Water (ちょすい):** while inhaling (hold Y), press the move button to take a liquid into the mouth; carry it; A spits it out; B swallows it. Using a transformation move (Rollout, Camouflage) while carrying deletes the liquid. **Spit level:** with liquid held, ZL + left/right sets a high or low level. High (default) makes a flow, good for waterfalls; low spreads the water evenly, good for ponds. Unlocked after Bleak Beach's story: Piplup's request (build a swamp tall-grass habitat, 沼地の草むら) brings Wooper, whose request teaches it — [Game8 JP ちょすい](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/768273)
- **Waterfall (たきのぼり):** while surfing, A in front of a waterfall shoots up it, so a waterfall works as an elevator. You can make a waterfall anywhere with Stockpile Water by spitting from a height. Chains into Glide off the top. Gyarados is on the north-west island of Sparkling Skylands; the hot soup bread is up inside a nearby building behind a rock (or cook one) — [Game8 JP たきのぼり](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/768277)
- **Glide (かっくう):** in mid-air press the button (Serebii and Game8's how-to page say R; Game8's summary table says B) to become Dragonite and glide. **It only slows the fall: you cannot gain height**, so you can't reach anywhere higher than where you start. You can steer left/right. If PP runs out mid-glide it continues until you leave the form; B, R or touching the ground ends it — [Game8 JP かっくう](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/768275); [Game8 JP 技一覧](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767409); [Serebii](https://www.serebii.net/pokemonpokopia/abilities.shtml)
- **Splash (はねる):** R jumps in place. The jump is high enough to climb a step of about 1 tile (1マスほどの段差なら登れる程度の高さ). **Uses no PP.** With it you can also jump during Rollout and Camouflage — [Game8 JP はねる](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/768202); [Game8 JP 技一覧](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767409) (that page's table says "B button", its text says R; the button depends on the "swap Run and Jump" setting)
- **Magnet Rise (でんじふゆう):** post-game. Magnemite is in the big hole under the rocket in Withered Wastelands; its request is the "factory storeroom" (工場の物置き) habitat, whose control panel (そうさばん) is opposite the office space on Sparkling Skylands' east island. ZR transforms. Fly freely; R up, L down. **Even at 0 PP the form doesn't end and you can still place items.** Controls: A place; Y tap = break item, Y hold = collect item whole; B leave form; + up = number of items to place; + down = facing; R-stick click = mode switch — [Game8 JP でんじふゆう](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/768199)
- **Dive (ダイビング, Ver. 2.0.0):** free for all players (DLC1 is needed only to enter Bubbly Basin). Requirements: version 2.0.0; Bleak Beach story done and its Environment Level at 5 ("Raise the Environment Level further"); Surf and Splash learned. Take Professor Tangrowth's "survey the unknown island" request, surf to the north-west island, give the collapsed Manaphy 5 seaweed. After that Ditto sinks to the seabed, never drowns, has **no time limit and uses no PP** underwater. Swimming past the map edge pushes you back — [Game8 JP ダイビング](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/803645); [Game8 JP 水中操作](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/803874)

### Inferences
- A planner can encode these areas (all guide-level, none measured): Water Gun normal = plus of radius 1 (5 tiles) on the target tile in front. Water Gun powered = Game8's "+2 orthogonal, +1 diagonal", most naturally a diamond of radius 2 (13 tiles), or reading "widened by 2" as orthogonal arms of 3 plus a 3×3 core. Leafage normal = 1 tile; powered = plus (GameWith "十字"). Rototiller = 1 tile, continuous while held. Rock Smash = 1 block per hit. Cut normal = 1 tile; powered = a forward line (3×6 per earlier research). Magnet Rise placement = up to 9 at once (see section 3).
- Rock Smash's normal-vs-powered split gives the planner a "breakable without food" flag. Blocks needing powered Rock Smash (or powered Rollout or smoothie-Surf): black rock, iron, gold, large stone, magma rock, hot rock, light stone, PokéMetal (GameWith list).
- No move gains reach or size permanently, so a planner needs only two states per move, normal and fed, not levels.

### Gaps
- No measured (community-tested) tile footprint for powered Water Gun, powered Leafage, powered Cut or smoothie-Surf. Serebii's cooking page says smoothie Surf "Hold for more vertical damage", which suggests a column, but the size isn't given.
- How the 4 food tiers (e.g. simple steak 350 vs vibrant steak 650 "PP healed") change area or speed, beyond Game8's "breaks hard blocks faster".
- Whether Headbutt has an area (it hits the tree in front).

## 2. Transformations, traversal, jump/climb height and reach

### Takeaway
Ditto's default form is "human" (ニンゲン状態). It cannot swim (deep water pushes it back to land until Surf) and has no jump until Splash. Splash gives a jump of about 1 tile and costs no PP. The traversal forms are Lapras (Surf; underwater with smoothie), Gyarados-style Waterfall (climb any waterfall), Dragonite (Glide: descends only, never gains height), Graveler (Rollout: fast, breaks blocks at or above its level, drains PP continuously), Magnemite (Magnet Rise: free 3-D flight, L/R down/up, post-game) and Dive (2.0.0: free underwater movement, no PP, no time limit). Normal reach is the tile in front, or the block above or below when aiming. Mouse mode reaches about 6 tiles. Magnet Rise reaches anywhere Ditto can fly.

## 3. Building tools, blocks per action, terrain tools, copy/blueprints, unremovable blocks

In human form Ditto places **one block per action** (inhale → spit) and breaks one block per Rock Smash punch, or a stream while held or rolling. Magnet Rise (post-game) **places up to 9 items at once**, with a count and orientation selector, horizontal/vertical facing and a **Replace mode** that swaps placed blocks for the held block. ## 5. Terrain-converting moves and exactly which tiles they affect

### Takeaway
- **Water Gun** (5-tile plus) revives withered grass, trees and flowers and moistens dry/barren soil (needed before tilling). It puts out fires, cools molten rock into footing, washes mud only together with a Water-specialty Pokémon's bubbles, and defuses glowing dig spots that are about to explode.
- **Leafage** grows grass on 1 tile, a plus when powered. Powered, it also grows duckweed on water, moss on rock/hard ground and vines on walls, and since 2.0.0 reverts farm soil to normal soil.
- **Rototiller** turns soil or turf into farm soil, 1 tile per press, and lifts grass and flowers whole.
- **Cut** harvests or fells.
- **Rock Smash, Rollout and smoothie Surf** remove blocks.
- **Stockpile Water** moves liquids and spreads water over a wider area than Water Gun.

### Cited Findings

## 7. The daily reset at 05:00 and other reset times

### Takeaway
At **05:00 local console time** the in-game date changes (not at midnight). Crops advance a stage. The Daily Challenge, the shop's "today only" stock and sometimes kit restocks refresh. One Dream Island visit, one Pokémon Center PP refill and one stamp become available again. Mosslax offerings expire (they last until 04:59). Glowing dig spots move. The Cloud Island backup-load limit resets. Jump-rope prizes reset (10 a day). Beequeen trades reset (4 a day). The Bleak Beach vending machine gives 1 drink a day. Other timers run continuously rather than at 05:00: berries regrow 1 per hour (up to 3), kit builds take 10 minutes to "next day", Tinkaton cuts a next-day build to 1 hour, dropped items and sparkles spawn randomly, and litter is checked about every 15 minutes (unconfirmed).

## 8. Offline progress, time travel, sleeping and skipping time

### Takeaway
Everything runs off the console clock, so closing the game doesn't stop it. Moving the clock forward (with internet time sync off) instantly completes kit builds, brings waiting habitat Pokémon, and runs any skipped 05:00 resets: crops, dailies, shop, Dream Island, dig spots. Japanese guides report **no penalty** (Game8: "not confirmed"; appmedia: only that you may miss Ho-Oh/Lugia flyovers). Moving it back has no benefit. Since an update (version not named), **seasonal events check that the console date matches the real date**, so events can't be replayed by time travel. The game autosaves to a single save, so there is no reload to undo it. No sleep or time-skip feature was found. During the opening tutorial the clock is fixed until Ditto first sleeps (earlier research, English guide summary).

### Cited Findings
- **Guide (Game8 JP):** changing the Switch 2 time while playing changes in-game time. Moving forward finishes long builds at once and makes Pokémon appear from new habitats at once. Daily items refresh when you pass 05:00. "Penalties: not confirmed at present"; no penalty event or effect seen. Turning time back has basically no benefit. Steps: Settings → System → Date and Time → turn off "Synchronize Clock via Internet" → set the date. On resume, if the date changed, you restart in Ditto's house — [Game8 JP 日付変更](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767333)
- **Guide (Game8 JP):** time travel also re-rolls the weekly shop day (step a day at a time until the shop day comes) and lets you visit another Dream Island the same real day — [Game8 JP 日付変更](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767333)
- **Guide (search summary only, appmedia):** time travel has basically no downside, except that heavy use makes it hard to catch Ho-Oh and Lugia, who appear at random and must be heard while playing — [appmedia 時渡り](https://appmedia.jp/?p=79804829)
- **Guide (gamepedia):** events now require real and console date/time to match (see section 6). Cloud Islands are not affected by date change — [gamepedia 毎日やること](https://gamepedia.jp/pocoapokemon/archives/851)
- **Guide (Game8 JP):** saving is autosave-only with one save, so you can't go back to an earlier point — [Game8 JP 取り返しのつかない要素](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767517)
- **Guide (search summaries, English):** save and close, move the clock past 05:00 the next day, relaunch, and next-day builds are done. No penalty reported; one guide advises saving before setting the clock back — search summary of [thegameswiki Day and Night Cycle](https://thegameswiki.com/pokopia/wiki/day-and-night-cycle), [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/pokemon-pokopia-building-wait-times-skip-switch-2-clock-2000676252), [Nerdschalk](https://nerdschalk.com/?p=467737), [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/?p=639075) (pages not opened; thegameswiki returned a bot check and the others are blocked from this container)
- **Guide (earlier research):** switching the console clock forward speeds crop growth — [Game8 farming](https://game8.jp/pocoapokemon/767639); [GameWith farm](https://gamewith.jp/pocoapokemon/545336)
- **Not found:** any bed or sleep action that skips time or restores PP after the tutorial; any in-game time skip; any explicit statement that berries (1 per hour) accrue while the game is closed.

### Inferences
- Builds and habitat arrivals complete after a clock jump made with the game closed (the documented method), so they are timestamp-based and progress offline. Crop growth and dailies are keyed to crossing 05:00, so a jump of several days probably applies several crop stages. Guides say "grows each date change" but none tested a multi-day jump: inference.
- Berry regrowth (hourly, kyu49) may be counted only while the area is loaded. kyu49 says the hour count "doesn't advance or resets" in some states, and Rowlet clocks tick only while you're in the area, so a planner shouldn't promise offline berry accrual.

### Gaps
- Whether multi-day skips stack crop stages, and whether going back in time undoes or freezes anything (crops, builds, the event lock). No test found.
- Whether berries, litter, specialty drops or Generate accrue offline.
- The version that added the event clock check.
