Pokopia Planner privacy

Updated 27 November 2026

Pokopia Planner is a free, fan-made planner for Pokémon Pokopia, at adamspecial.github.io/pokopia-planner. It isn't affiliated with Nintendo, The Pokémon Company or Game Freak. This page says what it does with your data.

On your device

Your plans, settings, item sizes and item reviews are saved in your browser on your device. They stay there until you delete them or clear the site's data.

If you sign in with Google to sync

Signing in is optional. You only need it to keep the same plans on more than one device.

  • One permission. The planner asks Google for one permission: to keep its own files in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive. It can't see, open or change anything else in your Drive.
  • What goes there. Your plans, item sizes and item reviews, and a small marker file. They stay in your own Google Drive. There's no server: nobody else receives them, the planner's author included.
  • Your account. The planner reads your Google account's email address and account ID from Drive. It shows the email so you can see which account is signed in, and uses the ID to keep different accounts' plans apart.
  • Sign-in key. Google gives the planner a sign-in key that lasts about an hour. It's kept in your browser on that device and is only ever sent to Google.

Until Drive sync is switched on, sync goes through Google's Firebase service instead, which keeps the same data under your Google account.

The planner's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

No tracking

There are no analytics, ads or tracking cookies. Your data isn't sold or shared with anyone.

Other services the page uses

  • Google Fonts, for the page's fonts.
  • Google's sign-in and Google Drive, only when you sync.
  • Serebii.net, for a few item and Pokémon pictures that aren't bundled with the planner.

Like any website, these services see your IP address and browser details when the page loads something from them, under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

  • On your device: delete plans in the Plans tab, or clear this site's data in your browser's settings.
  • In your Google Drive: in the planner, open Plans → Sync between devices → Remove from Drive. Or, on a computer, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Pokopia Planner → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • The permission: to take it back, open myaccount.google.com/connections, choose Pokopia Planner and remove its access.

Changes and contact

This page is updated whenever the planner changes how it handles data. For questions, open an issue at github.com/adamspecial/pokopia-planner/issues.
